# Indigen 0.1.31+40

Prepared 9 October 2026. The signed production bundle is built; it has not been
uploaded to Play or verified on a physical device. Compatible backend and the
website, dictionary and TribeStudio are deployed. Detailed evidence, commands,
rollout gates and rollback: [release handoff](../../operations/release-0.1.31-40.md).

Contributions now have account-scoped explicit draft recovery and stable retry
receipts. Supported correction forms retain metadata and revision history.
Downloads offers direct local-track playback with queue controls and current
account/subscription checks. Settings exposes the device's low-data preference;
optional video/image requests are avoided until deliberate media opening.
