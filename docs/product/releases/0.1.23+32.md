**Why this release.** Six commits merged to `apps/mobile` since 0.1.22 (31), and Firebase deploys cannot ship any of them:

- The music player can be minimised into a bubble, and closing it now really closes it (`ecf0955`, `ff49800`).
- The Kasem contribution form asks for a saying's literal reading and usage context, and a word's French meaning (`d6a2973`). The backend half went live with the 2026-09-23 deploy from `e90c5fb`.
- The advertising fallback (`ec795ca`, `d10ae13`, `ba63d26`). An existing ad position that has no Indigen World campaign can show a Google native advert to guests and free members.

It also fixes a bug that had been live since the mini-player first shipped: long-pressing a player-bar button broke it (uncommitted; see "Fixed - the player bar's tooltips").

This is the first release to carry the Google Mobile Ads SDK (`google_mobile_ads` 9.1.0).

## Full change list

### New - minimise the music player (`ecf0955`)

- **Minimise button.** The mini-player bar gains *Minimize player*, a down arrow (`keyboard_arrow_down_rounded`).
  - It sits with *Stop and close player*, after a gap, so that closing is never the button beside play or next.
  - It calls `MusicBarPlacementNotifier.collapse()`.
- **Previous.** The bar also gains a previous-track button where there is room (`miniPlayerShowsPrevious`).
  - The row needs 370 dp: artwork, gaps, five 40 dp controls and a 96 dp title floor.
  - With the bar's 10 dp margins and 1 dp border, that means screens about 392 dp wide and up.
  - Narrower phones keep previous on the now-playing screen, the notification and the lock screen.
- **The bubble.** `MusicBubble` is a circle exactly as tall as the bar (`kMusicBubbleSize = kMiniPlayerHeight`).
  - It shows the artwork under a 46% scrim, with four moving bars while playing or a pause glyph while paused.
  - The bars are `MusicEqualizer`, a `CustomPainter` behind a `RepaintBoundary`. It is mounted only while the music plays, so a paused or hidden bubble runs no ticker.
  - It deliberately does not use the accent fill of a floating action button, so the two cannot be confused.
- **Where it parks.** `MusicBubbleBounds.homeRect` is the bottom-right corner, raised by `kFrostedNavBarReservedSpace + kMusicBubbleFabClearance` (72). That clears the floating buttons six screens keep in that corner.
- **Dragging.** A pan moves it, clamped to the screen. On release it docks to the nearer edge at the height it was let go, stored as `MusicBubbleDock(onLeft, topFraction)`. A fraction, not pixels, so the dock survives a rotation, a keyboard or a split-screen window.
- **One widget, two faces.** `MusicPlayerDock`, mounted `Positioned.fill` by `MusicOverlay`, draws the bar and the bubble in one `Positioned.fromRect` and tweens between them.
- **Room.** While minimised, `MusicOverlay` stops inflating `MediaQuery.padding.bottom`, and `MusicInsetScope` publishes 0. Screens reclaim the bar's strip.
- **Back to the bar.** Tapping the bubble calls `expand()`. Starting a new queue does too, and so does `dismiss()`.
- **Playback untouched.** `music_bar_placement.dart` cannot reach the audio handler. The dock tests assert that minimising, expanding and dragging never call the controller.
- **Semantics.** The bubble is a button labelled "Playing: *title*. Open the player." or "Paused: *title*. Open the player."

### Changed - closing the player (`ecf0955`)

- **Close is a stronger statement than stop.** The X (*Stop and close player*) calls `MusicController.dismiss()`, which:
  - resets the session state;
  - calls `IndigenAudioHandler.clear()`, which empties the queue and the media item and then stops, so the notification and lock-screen controls go;
  - removes the saved resume point (`musicResumePreferenceKey`), so the next launch does not bring the song back.
- **No revival.** `IndigenAudioHandler.play()` returns early on an empty queue. A headset or car-stereo PLAY after closing no longer restarts the album, which just_audio would otherwise do because it keeps its source through `stop()`.
- **Stop alone is unchanged.** `stop()` still keeps the queue. It is what Android sends when a paused notification is swiped away, and the in-app bar survives that.

### New - Kasem contribution context, mobile half (`d6a2973`)

- **Form.**
  - Sayings (a dictionary contribution whose lexical kind is not `word`) get two optional boxes: *Literal English translation (optional)* and *When is this saying used? (optional)*.
  - Every dictionary contribution gets *French meaning (optional)*.
  - The values go to `submitCollectionContribution` as `literalTranslation`, `usageContext` and `frenchTranslation`, and only when they are not empty.
- **Dictionary.**
  - `DictionaryEntry` gains `literalTranslation`, `usageContext`, `frenchTranslation` and `authenticationStatus`.
  - The entry page shows *French meaning*, *Literal translation* and *When it is used* cards when those fields are present.
  - `authenticationStatus` is parsed but not yet shown in the app.
- **Backend.** Validation, the receipt, the canonical submission, the corpus area and the community/reviewed status were deployed on 2026-09-23 from `e90c5fb`, together with both dictionary websites.
- **Existing entries.** On 2026-09-23, none of the 349 published entries carried any of the three fields, so the new cards appear only as new contributions are published.

### New - the advertising fallback (`ec795ca`, `d10ae13`, `ba63d26`)

- **Eligibility.** `adsAllowedProvider` is the only client-side gate.
  - Guests and resolved free members are eligible.
  - Plus, Patron and Creator members are not: they see neither Indigen World campaigns nor Google adverts.
  - It fails closed while Firebase Auth, the entitlement or the backend benefits are unresolved.
- **Order in each position.** The positions and their cadence are unchanged (`kCommunityAdCadence` 10, `kExploreAdCadence` 6, `kCollectionListAdCadence` 5). Each position resolves in this order:
  1. an eligible first-party campaign, in the existing sponsored card or reel;
  2. the placement's Google Native advanced unit, only if consent permits a request;
  3. nothing: the row collapses to zero height.

  In Explore, a Google advert takes a whole page of the feed, centred on black and at most 400 wide, as a sponsored reel does.
- **No new formats.** There are no interstitial, rewarded or app-open adverts. Following, search, short lists that never reach the cadence, and account, payment and contribution screens stay ad-free.
- **Consent.**
  - Google UMP runs only after membership resolves to ad-eligible.
  - Mobile Ads initialises and requests only once `canRequestAds` is true.
  - A paid member gets one `requestConsentInfoUpdate` probe, with no form and no request (`ba63d26`), so a consent given while free can still be withdrawn.
  - Settings shows *Advertising privacy choices* when UMP says the entry point is required.
- **Privacy notice.** The in-app policy has a new *Advertising* section describing Google Mobile Ads, the identifiers and data involved, and suppression for members.
- **Build.**
  - `android/app/build.gradle.kts` refuses `bundleProductionRelease` without a valid production app id.
  - Debug builds are pinned to Google's sample app id.
  - The manifest's `com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID` comes from `${admobAppId}`.
- **New manifest permissions**, merged from the SDK: `android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION` and `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS` (Android's Privacy Sandbox). `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` was already present.

### Fixed - the player bar's tooltips (uncommitted at build time)

This bug was live since the mini-player first shipped. 0.1.22 already had *Pause*/*Play* and *Next* built this way, and 0.1.23 added *Previous*, *Minimize player* and *Stop and close player* with the same construction. It was found during the render check for this release's update post.

- **Cause.** `MusicOverlay` is mounted in `MaterialApp.builder`, above the Navigator (`lib/app/indigen_world_app.dart`), so nothing above the bar provided an `Overlay`. Each `_BarButton` is an `IconButton(tooltip: …)`, and in Flutter 3.47 a tooltip is a `RawTooltip` built on `OverlayPortal`.
- **What debug and test builds did.** `RawTooltip.build` asserts `debugCheckHasOverlay`, so every bar button drew as a red error box.
- **What release builds did.** The assert is stripped, so the bar drew normally.
  - When a tooltip was asked to show (on a phone, by long-pressing a bar button), `OverlayPortal.build` called `_RenderTheaterMarker.of`. That throws `FlutterError('No Overlay widget found.')` in release too.
  - The button then drew as a grey error box until the tooltip hid.
  - `main.dart` routes `FlutterError.onError` to `recordFlutterFatalError`, so each long-press was also logged as a *fatal* Crashlytics event, although the app kept running.
- **Fix.** `MusicOverlay` now wraps the dock in `Overlay.wrap`, which gives the bar's tooltips somewhere to show.
  - An Overlay with nothing showing hit-tests as empty (`_RenderTheater` has no `hitTestSelf`), so taps around the player still reach the app underneath.
  - The fix changes no other file in `lib/`.
- **Why no test had caught it.** Every music widget test pumped the dock under `MaterialApp.home`, inside the Navigator's Overlay.
- **Regression test.** `test/features/music/music_overlay_mount_test.dart` mounts `MusicOverlay` in `MaterialApp.builder`, as the app does. It checks three things:
  - the bar builds with no exception, with all five buttons present at 800 dp;
  - a long-press on *Minimize player* shows its tooltip and then lets it time out;
  - taps around the bar and around the bubble still reach the screen underneath.

  All three failed with "No Overlay widget found" before the fix and pass after it.

- **The bubble and the new close:** only 0.1.23 has them. On older apps the X still stops the music without clearing the queue or the resume point.

  - Long-press each bar button: its name should appear in a tooltip, and the button should stay intact rather than turning grey.
  - Crashlytics should record no "No Overlay widget found" event from 0.1.23.
  - Taps on the screen around the bar and the bubble should still work.
- **Saying contribution.** Submit a saying with a literal translation, usage context and French meaning. Follow it through review to publication, and check that the dictionary page shows the three cards.
- **Advertising, as a guest or free member, once AdMob is approved.**
  - Community, Explore and Collection show Indigen World campaigns first, a Google native advert as the fallback, and nothing on no-fill or offline.
  - A Plus, Patron or Creator account sees none.
  - Settings shows *Advertising privacy choices* where UMP requires it.
  - The European consent form, if a European test device or UMP debug geography is available, offers *Do not consent* as one tap.
