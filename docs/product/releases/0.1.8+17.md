## Full change list

### Fixed

- **The floating buttons sat a whole navigation bar too high.** Ask Kawuri on
  Learn, Collection and Contribute, and the composer on Community, all floated
  around a third of the way up the screen instead of just above the glass rail —
  146 logical pixels too high on a phone with a gesture bar, and every scroll
  view under them reserved the same dead strip at the bottom.

  The mini-player never told screens it existed. It inflated
  `MediaQuery.padding.bottom`, left `viewPadding.bottom` alone, and
  `musicInset()` returned the difference — a quiet mechanism with, it turned
  out, a second author. Every tab sits under the shell's `Scaffold`, which sets
  `extendBody: true`; Scaffold answers that by removing the bottom padding from
  the body outright and then setting it to the height of the bottom navigation
  bar. The difference the helper was reading was therefore the *rail*, always,
  whether or not anybody had ever pressed play.

  The overlay now publishes the number instead of implying it, through a
  `MusicInsetScope` above the router, and `musicInset()` reads that. The
  inflation stays — it is what lifts the rail, which sizes and positions itself
  from `MediaQuery.paddingOf` — but nothing infers a mini-player from it any
  more. The buttons rise when a song is playing, by the mini-player's height,
  and sit still otherwise: the same rule the rail has always followed.

### Changed

- **The Android build follows Play's app optimisation report.** Two of the four
  R8 settings the report looks for were not on.
  - **AGP 8.13.1 → 9.1.0.** Play asks for 9.0 by name; AGP 9 runs the optimised
    resource shrinker by default, which 8.x did not. Flutter 3.47 wants it too —
    its own dependency check warns below 9.0.1, and its template pins 9.1.0.
    Gradle 9.3.1 and Kotlin 2.4.0 were already on the versions that go with it.
  - **`android/app/proguard-rules.pro`, with `-repackageclasses ''`.** Classes
    R8 is already free to rename now move into one unnamed package instead of
    leaving the source tree's directory structure in the dex. Nothing held by a
    keep rule moves, which includes every component named in the merged
    manifest — the names that are looked up at runtime are the names that stay.
    6,421 classes came out of the dex's directory tree, and the bundle lost
    0.6 MB.

### Tests

- `test/features/music/music_inset_test.dart` — rewritten around the scope, and
  two new cases pump a real `extendBody` Scaffold with a rail-sized bottom bar:
  nothing playing reserves exactly the rail, and a song playing adds only its
  own height. Both fail on the old helper by exactly a rail's height.
- `test/app/shell_chrome_test.dart` — the composer sits clear of the rail and
  within one rail's height of it. On the old helper it stood 122 pixels clear
  instead of 8.

436 mobile tests. `flutter analyze` clean.

## Known gaps in this build

Everything listed under 0.1.6 and 0.1.7 still stands. In addition:

- **AGP 9 warns about two flags Flutter's own template sets.**
  `android.builtInKotlin=false` and `android.newDsl=false` are both deprecated
  for removal in AGP 10, and every plugin that applies KGP is named in a build
  warning. Neither is actionable here: the flags exist because eleven plugins
  still apply the Kotlin Gradle plugin themselves, and they come out when those
  plugins migrate.
- **This is the first bundle built with AGP 9 and with repackaging on.** Both
  are compile-time changes with no Dart in them, and the build is signed and
  installs, but neither has been exercised on a device across every plugin the
  app loads. Repackaging in particular only moves classes R8 was already free to
  rename, so a break would show as a `ClassNotFoundException` from something
  doing reflection by name. Watch Crashlytics on the first day of the internal
  track before promoting.
- **The bottom-inset fix is verified by tests, not by a run on hardware.** The
  numbers are pinned in two suites and the geometry is arithmetic, but the
  screenshots that started this were from a device and no replacement pair has
  been taken.
