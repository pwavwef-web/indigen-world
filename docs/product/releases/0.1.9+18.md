# Indigen 0.1.9 (18) — "Worth Keeping"

The first build that can take money, and the first that can be kept offline.
Three Google Play subscriptions, offline downloads for collection audio, and
the Play Integrity API reporting all seven of the signals Play Console lists.

**Why this bundle matters beyond what it adds.** It is the first release
carrying the Play Billing Library, and therefore the first whose manifest
declares `com.android.vending.BILLING`. Play Console keeps the whole *Monetise*
section — subscription products, Play Billing protection — locked until a
bundle with that permission has been uploaded to a track. **Internal testing is
enough; this does not need promoting to production.** Uploading this build is
what unlocks the console work in
[`docs/product/play-integrity-and-billing.md`](../play-integrity-and-billing.md).

## Full change list

### Added

- **Subscriptions, through Google Play Billing.** Three products —
  `indigen_plus`, `indigen_patron`, `indigen_creator` — each with a monthly and
  a yearly base plan. A purchase opens Play's own sheet; the token goes to
  `registerPlayPurchase`, which asks the Play Developer API what it is actually
  worth and writes `entitlements/{uid}`. That document is readable by its owner
  and writable by nobody, so a subscription is what Google Play told the
  backend rather than anything a phone asserted.

  No price is written anywhere in the app. The paywall shows Play's own
  formatted price, in the member's currency, after regional pricing and tax.

  Renewals, cancellations, grace periods and refunds arrive as real-time
  developer notifications on a Pub/Sub topic; a nightly sweep re-reads anything
  whose expiry has passed, because a notification can be missed and missing one
  always errs towards somebody keeping what they stopped paying for.

- **Offline downloads.** Songs and audiobook chapters can be kept on the
  device, with a per-tier limit, a Downloads screen under Settings, and a
  download button on the now-playing screen. The music queue substitutes a
  downloaded file for the stream automatically, so nothing else about playback
  changes.

  Downloads survive a lapsed subscription. The files are already on the phone
  and the audio streams free anyway; what lapses is the ability to add more.

- **A supporter mark.** Its own axis, deliberately not a `verifiedKind`. A
  verification mark says something was *checked* — a phone number, published
  work, standing as a custodian of Kasem. A supporter mark says something was
  *paid*, and it is drawn with a different glyph and explains itself in words
  when tapped. The kente ring is untouched and stays what it has always been:
  earned, never bought.

  The mark on a post's author stamp is checked against the member's profile in
  the Security Rules, so it cannot be claimed by editing one string.

- **The Play Integrity API, all seven signals.** Licensing, app tampering,
  device recognition, virtual devices, recent device activity, Play Protect
  state and app access risk. Firebase App Check already ran the Play Integrity
  *provider* and still does — that is the protection, and it is unchanged. This
  is the diagnosis alongside it: an App Check token carries none of those seven
  fields, which is why Play Console reported none of them as active.

  The device never judges itself. The token is opaque on the phone and is
  decoded server-side against Google. Every failure — no Play Store, no
  network, an emulator — resolves to "unavailable", which blocks nothing.

  It ships in **monitor** mode: verdicts are recorded and logged, and nothing
  is refused. A great many legitimate handsets in Ghana are rooted, sideloaded
  or on a custom ROM, and enforcing on day one against data nobody has looked
  at would lock out exactly the members this project exists for.

### Changed

- **Kawuri now has a daily allowance as well as a per-minute limit.** Twenty
  questions a day free, up to six hundred on a subscription, charged before the
  model call rather than after — a limit that only counts successful
  generations is a limit somebody can walk past by failing. The numbers live in
  one table on the backend, and the app asks for them rather than trusting the
  copy it shipped with, so an allowance can be raised in a functions deploy
  instead of an app rollout.

- **Adverts are suppressed for subscribers at a single point.** Explore, the
  Community timeline and the Collection all take their placements from
  `placedAdsProvider`, so the gate lives there rather than at three call sites.
  A fourth surface that starts showing adverts inherits it.

### Fixed

- **A stale generated plugin registrant broke release builds.**
  `GeneratedPluginRegistrant.java` is generated into `android/app/src/main/java`
  and gitignored. Running the test suite regenerates it *including* dev-only
  plugins, and the following release build reused that file rather than
  regenerating it — so `integration_test`, which is not on the release compile
  classpath, failed the build with "package does not exist". Deleting the
  generated file before a release build is the fix; it is worth knowing about
  because the failure looks like a dependency problem and is not.

## Not in this release

The console half. The subscription products do not exist in Play Console yet,
and cannot until this bundle is uploaded — see the note at the top. Until then
the paywall correctly shows "Subscriptions are not available on this device
yet", which is `queryProductDetails` returning nothing rather than a fault.

Play Integrity's seven services and Play Store protection are **not** blocked by
any of that and can be switched on now, on the testing track.
