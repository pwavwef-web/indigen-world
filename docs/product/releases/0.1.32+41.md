## Included changes

- Community's joined feed strip, searchable tags, Events/Community Updates support, visible Explore actions and clearer notifications.
- Downloads local queues, saved artwork, retry/remove controls, integrity checks and account/subscription checks. Saved files remain on-device after subscription expiry; playback requires current offline benefits.
- Draft recovery, durable submission retries, correction metadata/history and review/source browsing from the ten-improvement branch.
- Black appearance, shared motion, Contact Support, keyboard refinements and Low-data mode.
- Current main's Kasem grammar/spelling reference guides, illustrations and publication evidence. Older deployment content does not replace the current Studio design, expression flow or grounded Kawuri path.
- Wallpaper collections removed at the user's request, including the unpublished additions and copies in the desktop checkouts.
