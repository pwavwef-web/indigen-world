**Why this release.** The Indigen World websites moved to a navy and blue palette, while the app was still green. This release moves the app to the websites' blue, then makes the palette a choice:

- Indigen Blue (the default) and Heritage Green (the app's original palette) are free.
- Four more themes are a thank-you for Indigen Patron and Indigen Creator members: Tiébélé, Kente Gold, Harmattan Dusk and Volta Aurora.

## Full change list

### Changed - the blue look

- **Palettes.** `BrandPalette.light` and `.dark` follow `apps/website/src/styles/comitia-theme.css`:
  - light ground `#F6F7FB`, ink `#0F1830`;
  - links and selection `#2149B8`;
  - filled buttons `#2C66F5` (white on the site's `#2F6BFF` is 4.4988:1, just under AA);
  - dark mode is navy (`#0D1524` ground, `#8EB4FF` accent) instead of green charcoal.
- **Heroes and night surfaces.** Hero bands use the websites' `#0B1225 → #19327F → #2457D6` ramp. Kawuri, Explore's night ground, the Learn hero, the startup gate and the toasts were moved off hard-coded green.
- **Icon and launch.**
  - The launcher icon was regenerated from `tool/generate_app_icons.py`: navy field, white house, pale-blue bars and a cyan sun.
  - Android `splash_background` is `#0F1830` and `notification_accent` is `#2F6BFF`.
  - The iOS `LaunchScreen.storyboard` background is navy.
- **Green only as a signal.** `success` stays green for approvals, check marks and published states. Decorative uses moved to the accent:
  - post mentions and links;
  - "By creator";
  - the Now Playing artist link;
  - the profile Contributions stat and avatar fallback.
- **Categorical colours.**
  - `repost` is sky blue.
  - Community post categories: Question is blue, Language is teal.
  - On Collections, Music is teal and Literature is violet, so the tiles no longer share one blue.
- **Settings copy.** The Light and Dark appearance descriptions no longer mention green (en and fr).

### New - switchable themes

- **Catalogue.** `lib/core/brand_themes.dart` defines six `BrandTheme`s, each a light and a dark `BrandPalette`:
  - `blue` and `green` are free;
  - `tiebele`, `kente`, `harmattan` and `aurora` are `BrandThemeAccess.supporters`.
  - Heritage Green is the palette from `da8893b`, with one change: light `faintInk` is `#7F8783` (was `#8B938F`), to reach 3:1.
- **Theme-level tokens.** `BrandPalette` gained `highlight`, `heroDeep`, `heroMid`, `heroLit`, `nightGround`, `nightGlow` and `nightAccent`, identical in both halves of a theme, plus an `accentPlate` getter. Everything that stays dark in both appearances is painted from these, so a theme reaches it:
  - Kawuri (all screens);
  - the startup gate, including its painter;
  - the Learn hero and pattern;
  - toasts, snackbars and tooltips;
  - avatar rings;
  - media progress bars;
  - video covers.
- **Theme building.** `buildBrandTheme(theme, brightness)` is cached per pair, and `NightTheme` now follows `ActiveBrandTheme` instead of a fixed dark theme.
- **Choice.** `brandThemeChoiceProvider` is stored in `indigen_brand_theme_v1` and read before the first frame. An unknown id reads as blue.
- **Unlock.** `premiumThemesUnlockedProvider` is true for an active Patron or Creator entitlement, or when the backend's benefits say `premiumThemes`.
  - While sign-in or the entitlement is still loading, the last known answer (`indigen_premium_themes_unlocked_v1`) stands, so a supporter's app does not flash blue at launch.
  - A locked choice draws blue and is kept, so it returns with the membership.
- **Picker.** Settings → Theme opens `ThemePickerScreen`:
  - a Device / Light / Dark switch;
  - a live miniature of each theme in the current appearance;
  - a "Patron" lock badge on supporter themes, and a note with See memberships for non-supporters.
  - A locked tile opens a preview popup and changes nothing.
  - If a supporter theme is chosen but not currently open, a notice explains why the app is blue. The Settings row says so as well.
- **Membership.** Patron and Creator list "Exclusive app themes, like Kente Gold" (`BenefitKind.themes`).

### Changed - dictionary

- Entries with `contentKind: expression` keep the whole utterance as the headword, with `alternativeExpressions` as further renderings. Previously a comma inside a saying split it into separate renderings (`48516b3`).

## Deployment

Deployed on 2026-09-16 to `project-kassena-7e026`, from `229095f` (equal to `origin/main`, a clean tree apart from the version bump), by explicit `--only` lists with `FUNCTIONS_DISCOVERY_TIMEOUT=180`, before the bundle was checked.

| Target | Result |
|---|---|
| Before deploy | 104 exports, 100 live. The 4 exports not yet live were the only possible creates, and no function was live without an export, so no deletion was possible. |
| Functions, new (4) | `inviteExpressionContributor`, `assignContributorExpressions`, `saveExpressionAnswer`, `onContributorExpressionReviewed` (the invited contributor portal, `48516b3` and `1d3da64`). |
| Functions, updated (100) | Every other export, in five batches. The whole backend was redeployed because `subscription-catalog.ts` (the new `premiumThemes` benefit) is shared by Kawuri, media, subscriptions and billing. |
| First pass | Batches 2, 4 and 5 succeeded. Batch 1 had one transient `Failed to make request` on `confirmAdPayment`; its other 20 functions updated. Batch 3 was refused before it started: `onContributorExpressionReviewed` is `retry: true`, and a non-interactive deploy must confirm that with `--force`. |
| Retry | Batch 3 plus `confirmAdPayment` (22 functions) with `--force`: succeeded. `--force` could not delete anything under an explicit `--only` list. |
| After deploy | 104 functions live; the live list equals the exports, both ways. |
| Boot check | Unauthenticated calls answered with the functions' own "Sign in is required." (`confirmAdPayment`, `saveExpressionAnswer`, `inviteExpressionContributor`, `assignContributorExpressions`). `getSubscriptionOptions` returns `premiumThemes: true` for patron and creator, false for none and plus. |

**About the retry policy.** Before confirming with `--force`, `onContributorExpressionReviewed` was read. It recomputes the contributor item and training pair inside one transaction from the submission's current state, and returns early for submissions that are not from the portal. Retrying it is idempotent.

**Not deployed:** hosting (website, TribeStudio, admin), Firestore and Storage rules, indexes. The contributor portal also changed `firebase/firestore.rules` (`48516b3`), and its screens are in TribeStudio. Neither the rules nor TribeStudio were deployed in this release. Check both are live before inviting contributors.

**Effect on apps already installed.**

- **Themes and the blue look:** only 0.1.22 has them. Older apps are unchanged.
- **`premiumThemes`:** older apps ignore the unknown field.
- **Contributed expressions:** older apps still split a saying at a comma when showing it.

## Device and release checks still open

- **Theme switching on a phone:** in Settings → Theme, switch between the six themes in Light, Dark and Device. Check that pages, the Learn hero, Kawuri, Explore, toasts and the launch screen all follow. Rotate the phone and background the app while switching.
- **Supporter unlock, with a real Patron or Creator test purchase:**
  - the four themes open;
  - after the subscription lapses (or on a Plus account) the app returns to blue, keeps the choice, and the picker shows the notice.
- **Cold start as a supporter:** with Kente Gold chosen, kill and reopen the app offline and online. It should open in Kente Gold, without a blue flash.
- **Sign out as a supporter:** the app returns to blue.
- **Launcher icon:** check the adaptive icon under round and squircle masks, and the themed monochrome icon on Android 13 and later.
- **Membership screen:** check the new themes benefit row and its palette icon for Patron and Creator.
