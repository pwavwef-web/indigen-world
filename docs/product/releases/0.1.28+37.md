## What this release contains

Twenty changes, planned from a read of the code (the plan is summarised in the update post `apps/updates-blog/posts/2026-09-27-explore-and-my-space/`).

### Explore: scrolling and playback

1. **A reel that would not play after switching topic or feed.** The pager kept Flutter's default `keepPage: true` under a `PageStorageKey`, so a rebuilt pager restored its old page while the feed's own active index restarted at 0. `ReelFeedView` now uses `keepPage: false` and a plain key; a topic or feed opens at its top.
2. **Coming back to Explore.** Leaving the tab used to reset the window, the re-queued passes and the topic while the pager kept its index, so the member came back to whichever reel the pager sprang onto. Now nothing resets on the way out. Back within 15 minutes (`kExploreFreshStartAfter`) lands on the same reel; after longer away — another tab, or the app in the background — Explore starts fresh at the top, in one step. Tapping For you / Following while on it also goes to a freshly ranked top.
3. **The feed no longer reshuffles as it grows.** Once the member is past the first reel, the order dealt is written down (`exploreDealtOrderProvider`); later answers only add to the end (`keepDealtOrder`).
4. **Explore's own video query.** Community reels come from `watchVideoFeed` (`hasVideo == true`, `isReply == false`, newest first) over Explore's own window, instead of from the Community tab's feed filtered on the phone. Until its index exists the query falls back to the old source. The Community tab's window is its own again.
5. **Swiping back** finds the previous reel's player still open, paused where it was left. At most three players are open.
6. **Players are released** 30 seconds (`kReelPlayerReleaseGrace`) after Explore stops being on screen — another tab, or the app in the background — and reopen on return.
7. **Double-tap to appreciate**, with a gold heart where the finger was. It never unlikes; a burst of taps writes one like. Adverts do not respond. Only a tap on the picture waits for a possible second tap — the rail's buttons stay instant.
8. **The "Play videos automatically" switch** now also stops Explore opening the reels either side of the one in front.

### My Space: You · Membership · Settings

9. **You** replaces Overview and Profile: identity, bio and the Edit / Preview buttons in one header; the completeness card only while something is missing; Contributions · Approved · Points as the only counts.
10. **Your library**: saved words, saved posts, kept reels and downloads in one place.
11. **Adverts** left the tab bar and became a row under Your work. `ProfileScreen(initialTab:)` lets other screens open a tab directly.
12. **One submissions list** with All / In review / Needs changes / Approved filters. `MyContributionsScreen` is gone. The list now asks for the newest 50 (with a fallback until its index is deployed), and the counts come from Firestore `count()` rather than from the list, which stopped at 50 while every translated word is a contribution.
13. **Settings** lost the rows that live elsewhere (saved posts, downloads, member search); Community guidelines moved to About.

### Removed

14. The Community drawer's own **Sign out** (which skipped the push-token clean-up) and its **Your keeps** row. Its Settings row opens My Space → Settings.
15. **Team-only details** — the app signature and the Firebase environment name — are hidden in production until the version row is tapped seven times.
16. **Empty channels**: Apps, Shop and Heroes leave the Collection grid while nothing is published in them.
17. **Dead code**: Explore's illustrative "Preview reel" mode and its device-local like/keep store, and an unused local contributions repository.

### Other fixes

18. **Sign-out** always removes this phone's push registration first (`signOutOfApp`).
19. **Translate a word** is the first card on the Contribute tab.
20. **Saved words follow the account**, stored as `savedWordIds` on `learnProgress/{uid}`. A guest's words join the first account that signs in on the phone; words already reconciled with one account never join another.

## Backend steps (not done — each on explicit go-ahead)

The app works before, during and after each of these.

1. **Backfill `hasVideo`** on existing community posts, dry run first:
   `node services/functions/scripts/backfill-community-has-video.mjs`, then again with `--commit`. Only `hasVideo` is written; post triggers fire on create only, so no notification is sent.
2. **Deploy the Firestore indexes** after the backfill: `communityPosts (hasVideo, isReply, createdAt desc)` and `collectionContributions (authUid, createdAt desc)`. Deploying the video index before the backfill would hide older community videos from Explore until the backfill runs.
3. **Deploy the Firestore rules**: `learnProgress.savedWordIds` is bounded to 2,000 entries. Without it saved words still sync; the bound is what keeps the owner-writable document small.

No Functions deploy is needed.

## Phone checklist (before uploading)

1. Explore: swipe a few reels, switch to Music and back — the first reel plays. Swipe down one reel — it resumes without a spinner.
2. Leave Explore for a minute and come back — same reel. Leave it for 20 minutes — the top of a fresh feed.
3. Double-tap a reel — gold heart, one like, never an unlike. Tap the rail buttons — no delay.
4. Turn off "Play videos automatically" — only the reel in front loads.
5. My Space: You, Membership, Settings; the Community drawer's Settings opens My Space on Settings; Sign out lives only in Settings.
6. Save a word on one phone, sign in on another — the word is there.
