## Full change list - Music, reimagined

Plan: `docs/product/music-collection-upgrade.md`. Update post: `apps/updates-blog/posts/2026-09-24-music-reimagined/`.

### Music has its own navigation bar

- `MusicScreen` is now a hub with four tabs: Home, Search, Artists and Library. Audiobooks have Readers in place of Artists.
- The tabs sit in an `IndexedStack`:
  - each keeps its scroll position;
  - tapping the current tab again scrolls it to the top;
  - back goes to Home first;
  - hidden tabs have `HeroMode` and `TickerMode` switched off.
- The bar is the app's own `FrostedNavBar`, in the Music tile's teal (gold for Audiobooks). It gained:
  - an optional `accent`;
  - a per-item `motion`;
  - a per-item `badge`;
  - a `heroTag`.
- The app nav bar and Music's share `kAppRailHeroTag`, so entering Music cross-fades the bar in place instead of covering it.
- New routes: `/music` and `/audiobooks`.

### Home

- **Header.** A collapsing header (`MusicStageDelegate`) in the colour of the playing song's cover, or the newest song's before anything plays.
  - Along its foot is the Kassena wall-painting band, now shared as `lib/shared/kassena_pattern.dart` with Learn. The band drifts only while music plays.
  - It shows the newest song with a play/equalizer button, Play all and Shuffle.
- **Shelves.** Jump back in, Artists (the playing artist gets a turning ring), Browse by kind, New in the archive, and Every song with ads spliced in as before.
  - Browse by kind appears only when at least two categories have two or more songs each.
  - New in the archive appears only at 12 songs or more.
- **Loading and errors.** Loading shows skeleton placeholders. Errors use `GlassEmptyState`.

### Now playing

- **Opening.** `/now-playing` is a non-opaque `CustomTransitionPage` that grows out of the bar (or the bubble) by way of `nowPlayingOrigin`.
- **Hiding the bar.** While it is open, `nowPlayingOpenProvider` hides the dock without dropping its inset. This fixes the mini-player being drawn over the foot of the full player.
- **Background.** The blurred artwork, decoded at 64 px, over the song's colour, with a glow that breathes only while the song plays.
- **Artwork.** It scales between 1.0 when playing and 0.86 when paused. Swipe it to skip.
- **Controls.**
  - The seek bar thickens under a finger and shows the time in a bubble.
  - Play and pause morph into each other, previous and next nudge, and shuffle and repeat show a dot.
  - Pull down to dismiss.
- **Words.** A card opens a full-screen read-along with adjustable text size. The Up next sheet is restyled.

### Artist and category pages

- An artist's face flies in as a `Hero`.
- `MusicPageHeaderDelegate` paints the header in the portrait's colour, and the play button docks into the toolbar as the page scrolls.
- Category pages use the same layout (`MusicListScreen`).

### Mini-player and bubble

- The bar takes a wash of the song's colour, and the play/pause icon morphs.
- Swipe the title to skip.
- Long titles scroll while playing. They are measured in the font they are drawn in.
- The bubble shows a progress ring (`MusicProgressRing`).

### App nav bar and Collection

- **Nav bar.**
  - Each destination has a one-shot movement: Explore spins, Learn tosses, Community pops, Collection flips, Contribute turns a quarter.
  - While music plays, Collection wears a live equalizer (`MusicLiveBadge`).
- **Collection tab.**
  - Every Collection tile opens its page with `RevealPageRoute`, which grows the page out of the tile.
  - The Music and Audiobooks tiles show the song that is playing.
- **Removed.** The unused `MusicCollectionScreen` and `AudiobookCollectionScreen`.

### Colour and motion

- **Colour.** `music_tint.dart` reads a colour from each cover (`extractArtworkTint`: a 24-pixel decode, then hue buckets) and darkens it until white text passes 4.5:1. A cover that fails to load gives no colour rather than an uncaught error.
- **Motion.** `lib/shared/motion.dart` holds the shared durations and curves, plus `Entrance`, `EntranceGate` and `PressScale`.
- **Loops only while playing.** Everything that repeats runs only while music plays, and nothing moves under reduce-motion.

### Fixed along the way

- The now-playing background had drawn as a small blurred box. An `AnimatedSwitcher` with a loose stack was the cause; the render check found it.
- The nav bar labels now name the theme's font family outright, matching the rest of the app's text.
