A small release, and all of it is polish on things 0.1.6 shipped: the letterbox
around a reel, where a post's video sits, the top of somebody's profile page,
and how much of the screen a feed gives back while you read. Plus one new thing
that closes a loop the dictionary has had open since it shipped — you can now
say the word.

## Full change list

### New

- **A dictionary word can be said out loud.** The Contribute form asks a
  dictionary contributor to record the headword — record, hear it back,
  re-record, remove; 30 seconds at most, and entirely optional, because
  somewhere with no quiet room and no microphone permission to give must still
  be able to send the word. The review desk already plays any audio submission,
  so a reviewer hears the pronunciation before approving it. On publish the
  recording is copied out of the contributor's private prefix into
  `published-media/{entryId}/pronunciation` and its URL is written to the entry
  as `audioUrl`.
  - The play button on a published entry has been a stub since the dictionary
    shipped — it apologised for itself, because no entry had ever carried a
    recording. It plays now, and an entry that still has none says so in a
    sentence rather than showing no control at all.
  - `audioUrl` was also, quietly, the last fallback for the *written*
    pronunciation guide. An entry with audio would have rendered a download URL
    where its phonetics belong. They are separate fields now.
  - The mic permission was already declared for community voice notes; nothing
    new is asked of a device.

### Changed

- **Reels light their own edges.** 0.1.6 made Explore videos fit rather than
  fill — the right call, because cropping a landscape clip to a portrait screen
  throws away the sides of the shot — but it filled the resulting letterbox
  with the poster: the clip's own first frame, held still for the whole play.
  Anything that moved played inside a photograph of the moment before it
  started, and the seam was the most obvious thing on the card. The bands now
  carry the moving picture: the same texture the player is already drawing,
  sampled a second time, blown 18% past the card's edges so the blur has real
  pixels to reach for in the corners, blurred, and dimmed so the reel stays the
  brightest thing on screen.
  - No second decode and no second stream — it is one texture drawn twice.
  - A clip already the shape of the card skips the layer entirely rather than
    paying for a full-screen blur per frame for edges that do not exist.
  - Only the active reel holds a player, so there is only ever one of these.
- **Community attachments sit centred.** A post's media lived in the column
  beside the avatar, which starts an avatar's width in from one edge and stops
  short of the other — so an attachment read as pushed to the right, and a
  portrait video, the tallest thing in the feed, took the whole post with it. It
  now spans the card with matched margins. Writing stays under the byline it
  belongs to; the picture does not have to.
- **And they stop at three by four.** A phone shoots 9:16, and drawn at that one
  post was most of a screen — a feed of slabs with a line of writing between
  them. A single attachment is now held between 3:4 and 16:9, centred inside
  that box and cropped evenly from both ends. Tapping it still opens the whole
  thing, uncropped.
- **The bottom rail goes with the composer.** The Community composer already
  slid away as you read further in; the glass rail stayed put on the strip of
  feed underneath it, which is the half a reader actually loses. Both now move
  on one flag, so neither can be caught halfway, and both come back the moment
  you turn around. The rail slides rather than leaving the layout — the body
  already extends behind it — so not one row of the feed moves.
  - Untappable on its way out, so a destination is never changed by a thumb
    that was reaching for a post.
  - Any tab change restores it, since a scroll is otherwise the only gesture
    that could.
  - The connection banner follows it down instead of hovering over the gap.

### Fixed

- **The cover picture sliced the top off the profile photo.** Slivers paint in
  list order with the first one on top, so an avatar drawn in the header sliver
  and nudged upwards to overlap the cover above it was painted over by that
  cover — on every public profile, for everyone. The avatar is drawn by the app
  bar now, overhanging its own bottom edge, and fades out as the bar collapses
  so it is never left hanging over somebody's posts.

### Backend

- `services/functions/src/creators.ts` — `decideSubmission` carries an approved
  dictionary pronunciation out to the public path on publish, and writes
  `audioUrl` onto the entry. Idempotent: a re-publish keeps the URL it already
  has, and a failed copy is logged rather than failing the decision.
- `services/functions/src/published-media.ts` —
  `finalisePublishedPronunciation`, the dictionary counterpart to
  `finalisePublishedMedia`. A word is not a `publishedContent` record, so it
  needs the same Storage move and a different field at the end of it.
- Deployed 2026-08-30 to `project-kassena-7e026`: all 33 functions, by explicit
  name list, after regenerating `services/functions/functions.yaml` from a
  freshly built bundle and checking it against the exports in `index.ts` and
  against `firebase functions:list`.

## Known gaps in this build

Everything listed under 0.1.6 still stands — Paystack is on test keys, the
Paystack webhook is not registered, adverts are reviewed but not served, reels
have no public view count, and the unused Kawuri assets still ship. In addition:

- **The pronunciation copy step runs for the first time in production.** The
  emulator e2e suite covers the Firestore half only; Storage does not run there,
  so `finalisePublishedPronunciation` is exercised as a logged no-op. That is
  itself the design — media I/O must never fail a publication — but it means the
  first real dictionary publish with audio is the first time the copy actually
  runs. Check the entry's `audioUrl` after it.
- **Existing published entries have no `audioUrl`.** Nothing backfills; a word
  gains a recording only when somebody contributes one and it is published.
- **The ambient blur has not been measured on a low-end device.** It is one
  full-screen gaussian on the active reel only, and it is skipped outright when
  the clip fills the card, but the frame cost on a bottom-of-range Android has
  not been profiled.
