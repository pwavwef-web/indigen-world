## What this release contains

### New in 0.1.29: the language loop

The full design is in `docs/product/language-loop.md`. The update post is `apps/updates-blog/posts/2026-09-28-help-the-dictionary/`.

1. **Word prompts in Explore.** A card over the reel sometimes asks for the Kasem of a real open word from `wordQueue`, with the queue's example sentence and its Tatoeba credit. It appears first after 5 reels, then at most every 14, and at most 3 times a session. It leaves after 2 reels if ignored. **×** dismisses that word for good on the phone, and words already answered are not asked again. With nothing to ask, no card appears.
2. **One queue item per word.** The Explore prompt, dictionary search, a topic page and Kawuri all open `/contribute/word/<id>`. The id comes from the word itself (`queueWordIdFor`, the seed's own `wordQueueId`), so answers from every door reach the same item. Several members can answer the same word.
3. **"Help add this word"** appears when a dictionary search finds nothing for an English-looking query. It also appears on **topic pages** (Dictionary → Browse → a topic), which list the topic's verified words and the words people are asking for under it. A word nobody seeded is created by the new `requestQueueWord`. A word that already has a verified translation sends the member to the dictionary instead.
4. **Write first, sign in after.** A guest can write the whole answer, then tap "Sign in to send". The answer is still there after sign-in, and nothing is sent until the member taps Send. After sending, the member sees **"Submitted for review"** and "Follow it in Your submissions".
5. **Credit and training choices** sit on the form and are kept across words. *Credit me by name* is on by default; off shows "an Indigen World contributor". *Let it help train language tools* is off unless the member turns it on.
6. **The reviewer decides what an answer becomes.** On the mobile review desk (and the admin desk) the choices are: a dictionary word, a regional variant of a published word, an expression, an example sentence, a translation pair, or training material. Training material needs consent and is never published. Options the server would refuse are disabled, with the reason shown. A duplicate rejection names the word it repeats.
7. **Corrections.** "Ask for changes" now works for word-queue answers. In Your submissions the member sees the note and taps **Correct your answer**. The form opens with the earlier answer filled in, and the correction rewrites the same contribution, keeping its history.
8. **Practise with Kawuri** is available on dictionary entries and in a reel's context sheet. It runs a short lesson built only from published dictionary entries. A post's own Kasem is labelled "from the post, not verified". Each lesson ends with next steps: save the word, read the entry, answer a queue word, or ask Kawuri something else.
9. **Kawuri shows its sources.** Under an answer it links each verified entry it drew on. For each word it could not verify, it offers "Help add". It says when members' translations are still waiting for review, and it is never shown those translations.
10. **Measurements.** Ten Firebase Analytics events, `loop_prompt_impression` … `loop_lesson_complete`. They carry no member ids and no typed text.
