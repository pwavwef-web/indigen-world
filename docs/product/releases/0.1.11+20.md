Two things at once. The dictionary stops asking members questions that have no
answer, and a shared Community post finally lands somewhere real.

The first is the larger change even though it is the quieter one. The guided
word queue is fifteen thousand English words in frequency order, and ranks one
to seven are `the, of, to, and, a, in, is` - so every member who ever opened it
met, first, seven questions with no answer. There is no Kasem word for "the":
definiteness is a property of the noun, not a separate word. Those rows are now
answered once, as grammar, instead of being asked fifteen thousand times.

## Full change list

### New - the dictionary learns grammar

- **A sentence can be wrong without the word being wrong.** Rank 31 of the
  queue is the word *word*, shown in "My teacher put in a good word for me" -
  an idiom. A member had three options and all were bad: translate the idiom
  and poison the entry, translate the plain word and leave a reviewer unable to
  tell which they meant, or skip a word they knew perfectly well. A submission
  now carries `sentenceFit` (`fits` / `idiom` / `other-sense`) *alongside* a
  real answer, so the word gets answered and the sentence gets flagged. The
  control is pre-selected on "the sentence fits", so the median word still
  costs zero extra taps.

- **A flagged saying is offered a home.** After sending an answer marked
  `idiom`, the receipt strip offers "Record that saying too?", which opens the
  existing idiom contribution form pre-filled with the English sentence. No new
  backend surface - `parseCollectionContributionInput` already accepted every
  field it sends. The offer comes *after* the send, so the queue's rhythm is
  untouched.

- **Nouns carry their forms.** Choosing Noun reveals two optional fields:
  "Say it with *the*" and "Say it for many". Nobody is asked to name a noun
  class - almost no fluent speaker of any language can - so the class is
  induced server-side from the definite ending instead. Both fields are
  optional and nothing else in the form changed, so every verb, adjective and
  unelaborated noun costs exactly what it cost before.

- **The plain form is stated, not asked for.** The Kasem indefinite is
  invariantly the noun plus `mo`, which makes it a rule rather than per-entry
  data. It is derived wherever it is shown and never stored, so every noun
  already in the collection - including entries contributed long before any of
  this existed - gained its plain form with **no backfill**. A new Forms card
  on the entry screen shows plain / with "the" / for many.

- **`grammarRules`: a small closed collection beside the large open one.**
  About a dozen documents carrying what the word queue could never collect.
  Published rules are world-readable; drafts stay with staff, because a
  half-written grammatical claim about somebody's language is exactly the thing
  that gets read as settled. No client writes, ever - rules are authored by the
  project through `seed-grammar.mjs`.

- **Kawuri answers function words from the grammar.** `kawuri-dictionary.ts`
  discards `the, a, of, to, in, and, is` as stop words, quite correctly, and
  the consequence was that "how do you say *the* in Kasem" produced no briefing
  at all - so the model answered from memory. `kawuri-grammar.ts` is the same
  extraction with the filter inverted. The two lookups are exclusive by
  construction and now divide the question space between them with nothing
  falling through.

### New - sharing, Collection and Music

- **Shared Community post links.** Posts can be shared as
  `https://indigenworld.com/post/<id>`. Verified Android App Links open the
  production app directly, while the website now has a real `/post/<id>` page
  for laptops, browsers and phones without the app installed. The custom
  `indigen://post/<id>` handoff remains as a fallback when verification cannot
  apply.

- **A searchable Collection home.** The Collection tab now keeps a query box,
  filter chips, loading skeletons, clearer empty and error states, and tile
  sizing that does not shift when counts, labels or text scale change.

- **A Music library instead of one flat grid.** Music and audiobooks now expose
  artist shelves, artist pages, search, recently played items, track rows and a
  queue sheet. The now-playing screen can open the artist behind the current
  track and jump within the current queue without rebuilding it.

### Changed

- **Function words leave the word queue.** `retire-grammar-words.mjs` retires
  every row a grammar rule speaks for, recording `retiredReason: 'grammar'` and
  the rule id. Nothing is deleted and no counters are touched. Members now
  start on words they can actually answer.

- **The seeder can no longer clobber a repaired sentence.** `sentence` is a
  field the seed owns and rewrites on every run, which would have restored the
  original the first time a validator fixed one. A row carrying
  `sentenceOverride: true` now keeps its sentence and attribution while still
  taking a new rank or tier.

- **Tatoeba is credited on the Licences screen.** A new SOURCE MATERIAL section
  in Settings credits the corpus as a whole under CC BY 2.0 FR, alongside the
  per-sentence credit that already renders wherever a sentence is shown. It
  also states the boundary explicitly: community Kasem is not under that
  licence, and sentences written for this project carry no Tatoeba credit
  because none is owed.

- **Community media keeps its context in the full-screen viewer.** An opened
  photo now carries the author, handle, verification/supporter marks and
  caption over the image, and the byline opens the author's profile after
  putting the viewer away.

- **The subscription paywall waits for Play Billing to answer.** Startup is
  shared between callers, retry is available for recoverable empty states, and
  unavailable-plan diagnostics include the package id the build is actually
  running under.

- **Kawuri answers translation questions from the published dictionary.** The
  callable searches published dictionary entries before the model answers,
  gives the model only those attested forms to quote, raises the output budget,
  disables default thinking unless configured, and logs any future token-limit
  cutoff.

### Fixed

- **A question about a function word no longer poisons the dictionary lookup.**
  "How do you say the in Kasem?" was matching the primary phrasing, losing the
  captured term to the stop-word filter, and then falling through to the bare
  fallback pattern - which matched the tail of the very question already
  understood and sent Kawuri "how do you say the", "how", "you" and "say" to
  look up. The fallback is now guarded on whether a pattern *matched* rather
  than on whether anything survived filtering. Found by the new grammar tests.

### Contracts

- `lexical-entry.schema.json` carried a stale twelve-item `partOfSpeech` enum
  that had been contradicting the twenty-five ids in `lexical-kinds.ts` -
  missing `ideophone`, `classifier`, `postposition`, `prefix`, `suffix` and
  more. Corrected, and extended with optional `forms` and `nounClass`.
- `sentence-pair.schema.json` gains an optional `gloss` array with per-token
  alignment. **Groundwork only** - nothing writes `sentencePairs` yet. It is
  here so the next writer does not invent a competing shape.

### Website and backend

- Firebase Hosting now preserves `.well-known` app-link files, rewrites
  `/post/**` to the post page, and keeps route entry documents uncached.
- The website router understands dynamic routes and the dictionary reader uses
  the shared Firebase app helper.
- The shared-post link chain is covered by the website validation script.

## Deliberately not done

- **`NOUN_CLASSES` ships empty**, and `induceNounClass` returns `null` rather
  than a fallback class for every unrecognised ending. Kasem certainly has a
  noun-class system; this project has not yet written the inventory down in a
  form it can point at, and a plausible invented class would be published,
  taught, quoted by Kawuri and indistinguishable from a real one. A test pins
  the refusal. Nothing is blocked - real definite forms are collected from day
  one, which is the evidence the inventory has to be built from.
- **Three grammar rules ship with empty summaries** - prepositions,
  conjunctions and copula. They carry their trigger words, so those rows leave
  the queue, but the explanations are left for a Kasem speaker to write rather
  than guessed at. They are `draft`, so they are invisible to readers and to
  Kawuri.
- No sentence-pair producer, no validator UI for correcting an induced noun
  class (the `nounClassSource` field reserves the space), no dedicated grammar
  screen, and no backfill of `dictionaryEntries` - every new field is additive
  and optional.
