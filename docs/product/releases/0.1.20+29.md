---

**Why this release.** Kawuri has been a guide you could talk to: it could answer questions about Kassena culture and quote the Kasem dictionary. This release lets it **make and see**. A member can create an image or a short video from a description, speak a message in English instead of typing it, and ask what a photo, clip or recording shows.

**How it works.**
- All of it runs on Google's Gemini and Veo models on Vertex AI, reached only through Indigen World's own Cloud Functions. The app holds no AI credentials.
- The app shows a tool only when the server says it is available to that member.
- Every analysis keeps what Kawuri saw apart from what it is guessing.

## Full change list

### New - Create image

- Describe a picture and choose a shape: square 1:1, portrait 3:4, landscape 4:3, tall 9:16 or wide 16:9. The shapes come from the server's capability manifest, derived from the configured model, not from the app.
- An optional **reference picture** guides colour, composition or style. It is uploaded to the member's private temporary Kawuri folder and checked by the server. Live-tested: an image redrawn from a reference picture.
- A finished image can be:
  - saved through the system save dialog
  - shared, with an AI-generated disclosure attached
  - used as a **reel cover**, which starts a reel draft with the picture as its cover
  - used in a **contribution** as the artwork of a song or recording, with an AI-assistance note for reviewers
  - regenerated, edited and resubmitted, or deleted
- The model is `gemini-3.1-flash-image`, falling back to `gemini-2.5-flash-image` if the primary is unavailable. One image per request.

### New - Create video

- Clips are 4, 6 or 8 seconds long, in portrait 9:16 at 720p or landscape 16:9 at 720p or 1080p.
- A clip can have an optional "leave out" prompt and an optional reference picture to start from. Live-tested: a 4-second video started from a picture.
- **Who can make one.** Approved creators and members on the Indigen Creator plan. Everyone else sees why the tool is unavailable instead of a form.
- **It asks first.** Every video asks "Use a video generation?" before anything is bought, and the server refuses a request without that confirmation.
- **It runs on the server.** The video is made in the background, survives the app closing and backend restarts, and sends a push notification on the account channel when it is ready or has failed. It opens at `/kawuri/creations/{taskId}`.
- **No sound.** Videos are made without audio on purpose, as in TribeStudio: the model would voice a language that is not Kasem.
- **Model.** `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`; the Creator plan may also use `veo-3.1-generate-001`.

### New - English voice input

- The microphone in Kawuri's composer opens a voice message sheet. It states "Voice input currently supports English only." and explains why the microphone is needed before the phone asks for permission.
- Record, pause, resume, listen back, then **Use as text**. The transcript lands in the composer to edit, and nothing is sent automatically. Unclear words are marked `[unclear]`.
- **Limits:** 2 minutes and 10 MB. The length is read from the file itself.
- **Privacy:** the recording is deleted from the server as soon as the request ends.
- **Language check:** speech that is not English, Kasem included, is refused with "Voice transcription currently supports English only. You can still type in another language." The request must say English, and so must the language actually spoken.
- **Model:** `gemini-3.8-flash`, falling back to `gemini-2.5-flash`.

### New - Analyse media

- **What can be attached:** an image (up to 20 MB), a video (up to 200 MB and 5 minutes) or audio (up to 25 MB and 10 minutes), from files or the camera.
- **What to do with it:** Describe; Extract visible text; Transcribe English speech; Generate captions; Summarise; Identify objects or activities; Suggest contribution metadata; Explain possible cultural context; Suggest language or topic tags; Check media quality.
- **Follow-ups:** further questions in the same conversation continue the same analysis.
- **How answers are laid out:** in separate sections, so observation, interpretation and uncertainty never mix.
  - **Seen directly**
  - **Possible context · not verified**
  - **Text found**
  - **Suggested languages · not verified**
  - **Suggested topics**
  - **What cannot be known from this**
- A **needs community verification** banner is forced on whenever interpretation or a language guess appears.
- The model is told not to state as fact identity, ethnicity, language, ritual, sacred objects, place or history, and not to identify people.

### New - Recent and the creation library

- Recent on Kawuri's home shows the two newest creations live from their task records, for example "Image · Ready" or "Video · Generating". A percentage appears only when Vertex reports one, and Veo does not.
- **See all** opens the library with All, Images, Videos and Analysis filters. Each row's menu offers only the actions valid for that task's state.

### New - safety screen

- Every image and video request, reference picture included, is checked against Indigen World's own rules before anything is bought.
- A request is refused if it asks for:
  - real people or children
  - sexual content or gore
  - hate
  - realistic news or politics
  - sacred rites presented as documentation
- Every refusal comes with a plain-language reason.
- **Why it exists:** in testing, the provider's own filters produced a photorealistic image of a head of state being arrested with injuries.
- **Behaviour on failure:** if the screen cannot be read, the request is refused.

### Changed - Kawuri

- The tool carousel, composer buttons and tools sheet follow the server's capability manifest. A tool that is off says why: Sign in, Creators, Unavailable or Checking.
- Kawuri's confirmation dialogs (create video, cancel, delete) use the app's glass confirm card.
- Translation grounding: if Kawuri adds anything beyond a dictionary entry, that sentence starts with "Not verified:". Kawuri no longer contradicts or respells an entry, and it invites community confirmation. This is live on the server for every app version.

### Changed - contributions

- The contribution form accepts a file or artwork handed over from elsewhere in the app, staged as if picked, with the AI disclosure pre-filled.

## Device and release checks still open

- **Images:** sign in on a physical phone and create one. Save it, share it, use it as a reel cover, and hand it to a song contribution.
- **Video as a creator:** confirm the spend dialog, lock the phone, and check the push notification arrives and opens the video. Play, save and share it, and use it in a reel.
- **Voice:** grant and deny microphone permission. Record, pause, listen, send, and edit the transcript. Speak Kasem and confirm the English-only refusal.
- **Analysis:** attach a photo, a gallery video and an audio file, take a photo, and ask a follow-up.
- **Limits:** try an oversized file and an unsupported file.
- **Signed out:** confirm every media tool says Sign in and opens nothing.
- **Signed URLs:** open a finished creation on a phone. The emulator cannot mint them, and production holds the grant.
- **App Check:** before turning `ENFORCE_APP_CHECK` on, confirm billable callables accept the app's limited-use tokens.
