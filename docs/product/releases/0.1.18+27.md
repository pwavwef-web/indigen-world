---

The theme of this release is simple: **work should still be there when somebody
comes back for it.** A creator can close a laptop while a video is rendering and
find it under Your videos later. A draft can be reopened instead of becoming an
unreachable Firestore row. A quiet community post gets another measured chance
before it disappears down the feed. A published story remains a book even when
an old record filed it as a video.

The mobile app also feels more like the rest of Indigen World. Its daylight
palette now uses the ecosystem's indigo, plaster and terracotta; Collections
opens with stories about places; Literature has a real illustrated reader; and
the composer uses the system Kasem keyboard instead of keeping a second,
app-only row of letters alive.

## Full change list

### New - places lead into the Collection

- **Three place stories now open the Collection:** the Basilica of Our Lady of
  Seven Sorrows in Navrongo, Paga Crocodile Pond and Tono Dam. Each story is
  bundled for offline reading and carries its story source, photographer,
  original image link and licence on the page itself.

- **The opening carousel replaces the old filter chips.** The Collection still
  searches every portal, but its first screen now gives somebody something to
  read rather than asking them to choose between abstract states such as
  Published and Open.

- **The advertising position is preserved without interrupting the grid.** One
  served campaign can sit in the carousel when adverts are allowed; otherwise
  the app shows its own shop card. Members whose entitlement removes adverts do
  not receive either.

### New - Literature has an illustrated reader

- **"Why the sky and clouds are far away" is now a nine-page reading
  experience.** A published document can carry a page manifest, and the app
  opens those pages in a reader built for swiping and zooming.

- **Reading and downloading are separate actions.** "Read illustrated story"
  opens the page reader; "Open document" keeps the original PDF available.
  Existing document records without page images continue to use the original
  document action.

- **A Literature item is always treated as reading material.** Historical
  TribeStudio records could be categorised as folklore or storytelling while
  carrying video media, which filed films into Literature. New publications
  route video work to Video, and the mobile client filters old misfiled films
  immediately rather than waiting for a database backfill.

- **The completed Kasem edition keeps its publishing inputs.** The returned
  worksheet, individual illustrations, generation manifest, builder and
  publication receipt remain under `docs/content/literature/sky-far-away/` and
  `output/pdf/sky-folktale/`, so the edition can be rebuilt and audited instead
  of surviving only as uploaded files.

### Changed - the app uses the ecosystem's visual language

- **Deep Indigo now carries the brand in daylight.** Links, selected controls,
  focus states and hero panels align with the approved tokens already used by
  the Indigen World website, TribeStudio and Kasem Dictionary.

- **Terracotta is the action colour.** Filled buttons and other pressable
  accents use terracotta while indigo remains the identifying colour. Plaster
  cream and sand provide the page and muted surfaces, with stronger borders and
  text contrast than the previous near-neutral palette.

- **Dark mode keeps its green-lit character.** Hero gradients resolve by
  brightness: indigo over daylight paper and heritage green over charcoal, so a
  dark hero remains visibly separate from its page.

- **Shared gradients replace one-off green panels** across Ads, Collection,
  Dictionary, Explore, Learn, profile, settings and subscription surfaces. A
  future palette correction now has one place to land.

### Changed - writing uses the real Kasem keyboard

- **The app-only seven-key strip is gone from the composer and dictionary
  fields.** A "Use Kasem keyboard" switch opens Android's input-method picker,
  keeping the cursor in the field where the member was writing.

- **The switch reports what the phone is actually using.** Android now returns
  both whether the Kasem keyboard is enabled and whether it is the selected
  input method. The control refreshes after the picker closes and when the app
  resumes, so enabled is no longer mistaken for active.

- **Setup remains the first step when necessary.** If the keyboard has not been
  enabled, the switch opens the existing guided setup before offering the
  system picker.

- **Posts are no longer restricted to a Kasem pledge.** The compulsory "I
  confirm this is written in Kasem" checkbox, its recovered-draft state and the
  corresponding report reason have been removed. The composer now invites a
  post or question in plain language and leaves the keyboard available for
  Kasem when it is wanted.

### New - quiet posts get an honest second chance

- **The feed prevents immediate burial without becoming an opaque ranking
  system.** Its five newest posts never move. Up to three original posts between
  eight hours and seven days old, still below fifteen views, can be lifted into
  measured slots with at least a full run of ordinary posts between them.

- **Least-seen comes first and reshares are excluded.** A reshare has already
  been deliberately resurfaced; lifting it again would crowd out the original
  posts this rule exists to help. The rest of the feed remains chronological.

### Changed - photos can be cropped to the picture they contain

- **Custom crop is now the default.** Four corner handles define a freely
  resizable rectangle, with a mask and rule-of-thirds guide showing exactly
  what will remain.

- **The quick presets remain.** Original, square, 4:5 portrait and 16:9
  landscape still take one tap, while pinch, drag and rotate continue to work.
  Export applies the custom rectangle to the source pixels rather than merely
  changing the preview frame.

### New - notification history can be cleaned up

- **One notification can be deleted from its row** after a destructive-action
  confirmation.

- **The whole notification centre can be cleared** from the app-bar menu. The
  repository removes rows in batches of 400 so an older account is not limited
  to the 80 notifications currently shown on screen.

- **Firestore still owns the boundary.** A client may remove only notifications
  addressed to its signed-in user; it cannot create an alert or delete another
  member's history.

### Changed - My Space contains the account controls it names

- **Membership is now a first-class My Space destination**, beside Overview,
  Profile, Adverts and Settings. It embeds the real subscription manager rather
  than linking to a second screen.

- **Settings is embedded in the same rail.** The former placeholder tab and its
  extra "App settings" hop are gone, and the membership row has been removed
  from Settings because Membership now has its own destination.

- **The screens share page chrome and preserve their own scroll position.** The
  embedded versions omit duplicate app bars and reserve space for the floating
  navigation rail.

### Changed - community chrome gets out of the way

- **The Community title and For you / Following tabs now leave together while
  scrolling.** Posts can use the full viewport, and the new-post pill follows
  the compact header position instead of leaving a blank tab-height band.

## TribeStudio

### New - drafts and published open posts can be reopened

- **Save draft now has a way back.** A stored draft opens in the same submission
  wizard with its text, media, disclosures, permissions and attestations filled
  from the saved record. The page confirms when the draft was saved and Your
  work offers Continue.

- **A published open post can be corrected in place.** It keeps its document id
  and public link, while saving republishes the corrected fields. Campaign
  entries remain frozen after entry and work under review remains locked, which
  matches the Firestore rules rather than offering buttons that will fail.

- **Creators can withdraw their own eligible work.** A confirmation explains
  that a published post leaves Explore immediately. The submission stays in
  the creator's list, marked Withdrawn, so the act is visible and auditable.

### New - every generated video has a way home

- **Your videos lists the creator's latest 60 jobs.** Prompt, model, creation
  time, estimated cost and status are searchable and sortable; completed jobs
  can be downloaded or carried directly into a new submission, and failed jobs
  show the reason the provider returned.

- **A creator can close the page while generation continues.** A scheduled
  sweep advances queued and running jobs on the server. It uses a lease to keep
  overlapping sweeps off the same job and marks a provider-silent job failed
  after thirty minutes rather than displaying a permanent spinner.

- **Reload recovery reads the job before polling the provider.** A temporary
  provider or allowance failure can no longer replace an existing job with an
  empty builder and tempt the creator to pay for the same video twice. The
  deterministic job id is also written into the URL before creation begins, so
  a lost callable response still leaves a route back to the committed job.

- **Polling recovers after transient failures.** The browser asks every fifteen
  seconds while the page is open, reports a run of failures after roughly
  forty-five seconds, and continues trying. Reopening a terminal job reads it
  without consuming the refresh allowance.

- **Finished video transfer is streamed into private Storage.** The backend
  counts bytes while they pass, removes a partial object after failure and
  retries an import no more than three times. This avoids buffering a large
  generated film twice in a 1 GiB function instance.

- **Playback and download use short-lived, owner-only signed URLs.** This fixes
  browser CORS failures without creating a permanent public Storage token. The
  callable rechecks the job owner and output path before signing anything.

- **A Kasem recording can be made in the builder.** The existing browser voice
  recorder now feeds the lip-sync audio upload directly, alongside the file
  picker.

### Fixed - provider failures become outcomes, not endless spinners

- The poller now reads `providerTaskId`, the field job creation actually writes,
  while accepting the historical `id` spelling so already-stranded work can
  recover.

- fal's `COMPLETED` state is checked for an error payload before the result is
  fetched. A completed failure now becomes FAILED, including its readable
  reason, rather than a retry loop against a result URL that can never succeed.

- Runway and fal validation responses are unpacked into useful messages. fal
  queue URLs are restricted to the expected HTTPS host, and overly long Runway
  reference-image URLs are refused with the provider's actual limit.

- Retrying the same request remains idempotent, but a SUBMITTING or FAILED job
  with no provider handle can now be resubmitted because it never bought a
  generation in the first place.

## Also new across the ecosystem since 0.1.17

- **Admin and TribeStudio now share `@indigen-world/console-ui`.** Both consoles
  use the same application shell, command palette, data table and primitives,
  replacing their separate collections of near-identical controls.

- **Creator guidance is one versioned contract.** TribeStudio's Help and
  Guidelines pages read expanded creator guidance shared through the contracts
  package, so review expectations and contributor instructions do not drift
  into separate copies.

- **The public website has new page artwork and a tester reward claim flow.**
  The form surfaces backend responses and its intake has emulator coverage.

- **The dictionary is now named Kasem Dictionary** in its page and install
  metadata, and Firebase Hosting routes it through the `kasem-dictionary`
  target.

- **Mobile tester claims and startup messaging were repaired** so long
  preference content lays out correctly and a form response is visible instead
  of appearing to do nothing.

## Deployment requirements

The app and Studio changes are not complete until their server-side half is
live. Deploy and verify, in a controlled order:

1. Firestore rules and the new `studioVideoJobs(ownerUid, createdAt DESC)`
   index, which allow the owner-scoped video history and notification deletion.
2. Cloud Functions, including `getStudioVideoPlaybackUrl` and
   `sweepStudioVideoJobs`, plus the publication routing and withdrawal trigger
   changes.
3. TribeStudio, whose new job recovery, edit and withdrawal UI depends on those
   rules and functions.
4. The mobile AAB only after the final source is committed, pushed and rebuilt
   from a tree that passes the production-main check.
