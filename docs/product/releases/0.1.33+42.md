# Indigen World 0.1.33 (42) — "Make It. Find It. Pass It On."

Release record prepared **17 October 2026**. Android version name `0.1.33`,
version code `42`, following [0.1.32 (41)](0.1.32+41.md).

An expression needs context. A film needs a final cut. A handmade object needs
a way to reach its buyer. This release connects more of that work: making,
finding and passing something on, with drafts, review and recovery along the way.

This release adds a dedicated Shop, shared artwork uploads, actionable
notifications, collection discovery improvements and a native mobile video
editor. It also carries forward the Admin, contributor, rewards, correspondence,
website, dictionary and Labs changes merged since the previous Android release.
The change list below covers that released source, rather than unrelated work
still present in the development checkout.

**Availability:** the signed production Android bundle is built and verified;
it has **not been uploaded to Google Play**. The affected backend, Admin and
public website were deployed and verified on 17 October. Checkout, rich push,
comment ads and mobile video export remain gated. An Apple release has not
been built or signed. Earlier platform deployments are identified separately.

## Full change list

### Shop navigation, products and saved items

- **A dedicated four-tab Shop.** Home, Categories, Cart and Profile replace the
  previous compact collection shop. Product, category, checkout and order routes
  open within the same experience, including supported deep links.
- **Product discovery.** Search, category, maker, availability and price filters
  work with explicit sort choices and cursor pages of 24 products. Product detail
  includes the image gallery, variant selection and related products.
- **Fixed-price and inquiry products are distinct.** Physical products can use
  configured checkout; quote-only items retain an inquiry path. A legacy missing
  or nonpositive price does not silently become a payable product. Paid digital
  goods are not introduced through the physical-goods payment path.
- **Saved and recently viewed items.** Members can return to products from the
  Shop profile and saved-item views. The guest cart and saved items persist
  locally; signed-in cart and wishlist state are stored for the account.
- **Guest-to-account recovery.** A stable merge key prevents the same guest cart
  being added repeatedly during sign-in retries. Account changes keep each
  account's state separate.
- **Cart controls.** Quantity changes, item removal and variant-specific lines
  are reflected in the cart. The server recalculates checkout amounts rather
  than accepting a displayed client total.

To use it, open **Collections → Shop**, choose a category or product, then save
it or add an available variant to the cart. Product availability depends on real
catalogue configuration. Checkout currently remains closed.

### Checkout, payments, inventory and refunds

- **Server-owned totals and order snapshots.** Checkout captures the purchased
  items, prices, address and delivery choice, with configured delivery charges,
  tax and discounts. Order records keep these snapshots rather than changing
  when somebody later edits a product or address.
- **Hosted GHS payments.** The payment integration supports Paystack's hosted
  card/mobile-money flow. Secret keys remain on the server. A payment purpose
  separates shop purchases from other existing payment workflows.
- **Stock reservations.** Checkout reserves variant stock transactionally for
  30 minutes. Reconciliation runs every ten minutes in bounded batches of 100.
  Concurrent checkouts cannot independently spend the same available quantity.
- **Stable payment references.** Verification checks reference, amount,
  currency, email, purpose and order ownership. A provider redirect supplies a
  lookup reference; it never proves payment.
- **Duplicate settlement protection.** Repeated webhooks allocate stock once.
  A delayed duplicate after a partial refund cannot reset the refund state or
  allocate the purchased stock again.
- **Late payments require resolution.** A settled payment for a cancelled order
  or unavailable stock becomes `refund_required`; it cannot proceed to dispatch.
- **Cart reconciliation.** Verified settlement subtracts the purchased cart
  quantities once, preserving additional quantities added after checkout.
- **Cancellation releases reservations once.** Cancellation and reservation
  expiry cannot return the same held stock repeatedly.
- **Finance-controlled refunds.** Refundable amounts are reserved before a
  provider operation, with stable refund references and reconciliation of
  uncertain outcomes. Another operator cannot refund the same reserved amount.
  An uncertain provider response must be reconciled using its existing reference.
- **Restocking is explicit.** Refunding money does not assert that returned
  goods were inspected or received. Staff make a separate inventory adjustment
  with a reason when restocking is appropriate.
- **Payment and fulfillment have separate states.** A payment confirmation is
  not a delivery confirmation, and an administrative action cannot imply that
  the provider paid or refunded money without verification.

The integration is deployed, but no real payment or refund was performed during
this release. Staff must finish GHS settings, support contact, returns and
delivery policies, coverage and actual stock, then complete provider acceptance
checks before enabling checkout.

### Customer orders and store administration

- **Shop profile.** Customers have saved addresses, saved products, order
  history, status and support paths.
- **Order requests.** Customers can raise cancellation or return requests.
  Staff resolution records the outcome and explanation with order history.
- **Product management.** Admin Shop contains products, categories, variants,
  SKU stock, gallery/cover ordering, publication controls and scheduled products.
  A future scheduled product stays hidden from older readers until its due time.
- **Inventory management.** Stock adjustments carry reasons and audit history;
  thresholds make low-stock work visible.
- **Store operations.** Settings, delivery zones, fees, pickup options, policies,
  orders, customer history and the implemented promotion controls share the
  Shop workspace. Finance operations use the dedicated Shop Finance desk.
- **Scoped staff access.** Server-issued `shopScopes` can limit catalogue,
  fulfillment, customer-data and finance work. Finance additionally requires the
  finance claim or super-admin authority. A client profile field grants no role.
  Existing admins without explicit scopes retain their legacy broad access.
- **Honest exports.** The CSV action exports the loaded 50-record page and says
  so. Text cells are protected against spreadsheet-formula execution; numeric
  amounts remain numeric. This is not an unlimited full-history export.
- **Additive migration.** The release migration was reviewed in dry-run mode
  and applied to the one existing production product. Unknown inventory starts
  at zero. Historical request-only `shopOrders` remain untouched; paid orders use
  the separate V2 path. Existing external image URLs were not downloaded.

Staff begin at **Admin → Shop**. Store configuration and stock must describe the
actual operation; screenshot products, customers and orders were not seeded.

### Shared image uploads and collection artwork

- **One media picker.** Admin supports direct upload, existing media-library
  selection and compatible image links through the shared picker.
- **Connected artwork fields.** The picker is used for shop products/categories,
  hero portraits, app icons/screenshots and audiobook covers. The collection
  artwork desk also manages published music, literature, audio and video covers.
- **Useful metadata.** Alt text, rights, credits, focal points, cover choice and
  ordering stay with the artwork. Processing/pending state is visible.
- **Private originals.** Admin-owned originals are immutable and limited to
  10 MB. Declared MIME type, actual byte signature and real decoder output must
  agree for JPEG, PNG or WebP. Images must be at least 32 pixels in dimension
  and no more than 40 megapixels.
- **Display derivatives.** Processing strips metadata and creates JPEG display
  and thumbnail versions bounded to 1600 and 480 pixels respectively. Published
  derivatives are used for display; private originals are retained separately.
- **No arbitrary server-side fetching.** A supplied external URL does not grant
  the processing worker permission to fetch an arbitrary destination.
- **Upload recovery.** Current transfers show resumable progress. After a
  reload, the picker can recover pending server assets; an unfinished local
  byte upload still needs the source file to be selected again.
- **Bounded work and cleanup.** Each admin can begin 100 uploads per hour.
  Image processing uses at most four 1 GiB workers, concurrency one. Seven-day
  abandoned-upload cleanup processes bounded batches and excludes published or
  shared assets, retaining uncertain legacy references conservatively.
- **Production region corrected.** The image processor runs in `us-east1` to
  match the existing Storage bucket; the other scoped functions use `us-central1`.

Contributor audio, video and document pipelines remain type-specific. Licensed
offline place-story photographs continue to ship as application assets.

### Notification inbox, actions and delivery

- **Clearer inbox cards.** The refreshed inbox can show the relevant post image,
  message preview and actions, alongside order notifications and existing
  notification filters.
- **Canonical destinations.** Payload version 2 identifies the actual target.
  A reply notification opens the reply's post rather than assuming that every
  action belongs to the root of the thread.
- **Retry-safe Like and Reply.** Like sets the requested state; an inline reply
  carries a stable identifier. Repeated delivery or a retry does not create
  repeated effects.
- **Authorization is checked at action time.** Recipient, canonical target,
  blocks, deleted/revoked content and active public-community membership are
  checked on the server before a post action is accepted.
- **Offline actions belong to an account.** The bounded queue holds at most 50
  actions for seven days. Actions drain after the intended account authenticates
  or opens the inbox. Logging out cannot replay one member's action as another.
- **Delivery states are precise.** Provider acceptance, opening and reading are
  separate events. Acceptance by the push provider is not reported as proof of
  device display or a member reading the message.
- **Bounded retries.** Dispatch uses leases, exponential backoff and at most
  four attempts. Invalid device tokens are pruned. Expired leases recover, while
  partial provider acceptance is recorded rather than resending blindly to all
  devices.
- **Quiet hours.** Preferences use an IANA timezone and handle daylight-saving
  transitions. Order updates bypass quiet hours. Promotions require opt-in when
  the message is dispatched.
- **Android alert handling.** Rich mode has app-rendered alerts for foreground,
  background and supported terminated states. Stable SHA-256-derived 31-bit IDs
  avoid process-dependent alert identifiers. Image downloads are bounded to
  5 MB, hidden-preview preferences are respected, and successful mark-read
  removes the OS alert. Existing system notification display remains the
  release fallback while rich mode is disabled.
- **Apple implementation prepared.** Categories, recipient-scoped action
  recovery and foreground local display are wired alongside APNs background
  display. The NotificationService extension permits approved HTTPS Google
  Storage image hosts, refuses redirects, caps images at 5 MB and preserves the
  text notification on failure. Target references are prepared across the
  existing build configurations.

Rich push and Apple rich media are disabled in production. Apple wiring checks
are structural; no Xcode compilation, signing or physical-device push behavior
was verified on this Windows machine. Force-stop, denied permissions and OS
throttling remain normal delivery limits.

### Notification campaigns and staff messaging

- **Saved-version review.** The Admin composer saves a revision for review
  before a campaign is queued. Staff can test to themselves, schedule, cancel
  and inspect history and audit records.
- **Separate broadcast permission.** Broadcasting requires the server-issued
  broadcast claim or super-admin authority; ordinary admin access is insufficient.
- **Bounded fan-out.** Dispatch pages 100 profiles at a time and processes up to
  ten active campaigns per tick, applying preferences at dispatch.
- **Verified with fixtures.** Browser checks covered draft review, test-self
  queueing and cancellation through synthetic services. No production campaign,
  self-test message or community broadcast was sent for this release.

### Collection discovery and specialist readers

- **Stable discovery sessions.** Bounded candidate sets are frozen for a
  session, with qualified visibility, recent-history signals, editorial pins
  and diversity. Explicit sorts remain stable while browsing the loaded set.
- **Collection search and paging.** Supported published-content collections
  retain their specialized detail routes while gaining discovery, search,
  explicit sorting and paged loading where implemented.
- **Literature.** Stories, poems, proverbs, riddles and actual additional
  categories retain their tabs and reader paths, including illustrated details
  and PDF documents.
- **Apps.** Search, category filters and editorial/name ordering lead to app
  details, platform links and zoomable screenshots.
- **Heroes.** Search, field filters and editorial/name ordering lead to the
  existing biography detail views.
- **Music and music video.** Existing listening, player queues and creator
  details are preserved. The artwork desk changes cover management without
  replacing contributor media uploads.
- **Audiobooks.** Narration, listening, downloads and reader behavior remain
  available through their existing specialist workflows.
- **Dictionary and names.** Kasem spelling, senses, dialect/search, saved words,
  validator controls and name/handle requests retain their existing behavior.
- **Place stories.** Licensed offline carousels, source credits and careful
  oral-tradition wording remain in the packaged experience.

Heroes and Apps currently read a bounded 100 records. Explicit collection sorts
operate on bounded loaded candidates; discovery history and ranking are not
universal across every subtype or device. The legacy published-word stream still
loads the small published dictionary. These limits are not infinite pagination.

### Comment sponsorship and Indigen-owned campaigns

- **A dedicated comment placement.** `post_comments` supports one compact
  sponsorship placement per thread, separate from organic comment documents and
  comment counts. Existing membership and consent gates still apply.
- **Explicit fallback.** Eligible first-party house content is considered first,
  then a dedicated native ad unit, then no placement. Existing Collection,
  Explore or Community ad units are not reused for this surface.
- **Real native ad layout.** The Android native factory uses SDK assets,
  MediaView, a minimum 120 dp media area, a 48 dp CTA and AdChoices. It reports
  measured natural height to Flutter so required content is not clipped into a
  fixed comment-sized box. Click handling remains with the SDK.
- **Release configuration checks.** An optional comment unit must be distinct
  from existing units and belong to the same publisher. Sample IDs are refused
  in release configuration.
- **Reserved first-party identity.** House campaigns use a server-owned
  identity linked to the verified Indigen organisation. They reuse the existing
  roof/bars/sun logo and approved Indigen destinations, with separate house
  telemetry rather than fabricated advertiser spending or payments.
- **Admin controls.** The House Ads workspace provides draft, review and
  campaign controls. Missing verified organisation configuration blocks identity
  creation; no unrelated profile is overwritten.

The production organisation ID and real dedicated AdMob unit remain
unconfigured, and comment ads default off in the Android release. No new live
ad unit was created. Device SDK, consent, no-fill, video-media and layout checks
remain necessary before enabling this placement.

### Native mobile video editor and draft recovery

- **Native entry from creation flows.** Community and reel video selection open
  the Flutter editor. Using the original clip remains an explicit choice. The
  editor is part of the application rather than an embedded web editor.
- **Versioned projects.** A shared mobile project/timeline contract preserves
  scenes and edits. Private account/project directories hold original media and
  atomic draft JSON; source uploads are sequential and recoverable.
- **Scene editing.** Import, reorder, trim, split, undo and redo are available,
  alongside fit/fill, aspect/crop, rotation and shared visual looks.
- **Text and captions.** Timed text/captions, SRT/VTT input and transcript paths
  are supported. Flutter rasterizes text/caption PNG layers for the existing
  worker, so mobile fonts and layout do not depend on browser-only artifacts.
- **Audio and overlays.** The project supports stickers, licensed music, voice
  recording/upload, volume, fades, muting and ducking. Trim, split and speed
  changes preserve the corresponding audio timing, including `atempo` handling.
- **Shared rendering infrastructure.** Mobile uses the existing TribeStudio
  assets, music/sticker catalogues, render service and FFmpeg pipeline. Shared
  look matrices keep supported looks aligned.
- **Preview boundaries are visible.** Local source preview supports fullscreen.
  The complete audio/sticker/transition mix is inspected through rendered output.
- **Render operations.** Progress, retry and cancellation are connected to
  server render state. Original media and drafts remain available when a render
  fails or the workflow is interrupted.
- **Output validation before posting.** Handoff requires the owner, latest
  revision, canonical timeline fingerprint and a successful non-draft output.
  Undo keeps revision counters monotonic. Normal audience and moderation checks
  remain part of posting.
- **Android picker recovery.** Lost picker results return through the editor
  path after the creation draft has been saved. Community and reel drafts are
  scoped to the account.
- **Explicit resource limits.** Each source is capped at 300 MB; a mobile
  project at 30 scenes and five minutes. The existing service allows 40 render
  starts per hour, three active exports per member, six concurrency-one workers
  and a 1.5 GB input budget. Retention keeps exports for 30 days and the newest
  three per project. Existing AI-generation entitlements are unchanged.
- **Unsupported projects remain recoverable.** Unsupported web project
  contracts are rejected and preserved rather than silently converted or lost.

The basic editor and drafts are included in the bundle. Mobile server rendering
defaults off until real-device interrupted-render, final preview and posting
checks pass. Real worker tests produced playable outputs; that does not establish
a successful production export/post journey on a phone.

### Typography, mobile layout and payment-return links

- **Bundled Noto Sans.** Regular and bold fonts provide consistent Kasem
  diacritics and metrics. The complete SIL Open Font Licence is explicitly
  included as a release asset.
- **Large text and dark appearance.** Actual changed Flutter screens were
  exercised at 1.8× text scale and in dark appearance. This is targeted screen
  coverage, not a complete accessibility certification.
- **Creation controls stay reachable.** The Community composer includes the
  earlier fix that keeps it above the navigation area.
- **Existing branding retained.** The app/public PNG mark is rendered from the
  existing roof/bars/sun SVG geometry.
- **Checkout return page.** `/shop/payment-complete` provides a mobile-friendly,
  noindex return page with app-opening and manual recovery guidance. Android,
  custom-scheme and Apple association paths include the supported Shop return.
- **Untrusted redirect parameters are filtered.** Only a canonical
  `shop_<40 hexadecimal characters>` reference from the supported reference
  fields is forwarded. Forged status and unknown parameters are discarded;
  invalid references fall back to Shop. The app fetches authenticated order
  state, and the server verifies payment separately.
- **Manual recovery remains available.** Customers can open
  **Shop → Profile → Orders** when an app-opening link cannot complete.

### Guided contributor studio and Kasem spelling assistance

- **Four editing sections.** Understand, Translate, Context and Check & send
  organize expression work, with freely accessible sections and persistent phone
  source/navigation controls.
- **A useful home dashboard.** Next work, approved/pending counts, participation,

  Returned work retains priority.

  explorer, jump/slide obstacles, sparks, three hearts, brief hit immunity,
  bounded difficulty, recycled scenery and a smoothed chase camera. Controls
  support keyboard, touch swipes and touch buttons, with pause/visibility handling.

  and distance survive checkpoint continuation. The existing 100-point game
  bonus follows a successful server receipt once; it is not cash or spendable
  contributor points.
- **Stale-word protection.** New clients send `expectedWordId`, so an answer
  cannot silently attach to a different queue word. Recovery reloads the saved
  prompt and requires deliberate reuse of previous writing. Old saved runs and
  clients retain compatibility.

  contribution bonus and preserves an existing account checkpoint gate.
- **Blue experiment palette.** Word Trail, Culture Quest and experiment preview
  accents share the final blue treatment.
- **Provenance and operating limits.** Procedural art and synthesized audio are
  original fictional work, with the Three.js licence bundled. Word Trail remains
  alpha and requires WebGL 2. Unsent in-game writing does not survive reload,
  exit or account changes; saved checkpoints remain on the backend. Real-phone
  performance, iOS/Safari keyboards and physical-device audio remain unverified.

## Recovery and rollback

- Disk-full Android attempts were discarded and the final bundle reverified.
  Older release bundles and the latest debug APK were preserved. Generated
  mobile output, npm cache, obsolete NDK versions and downloaded test-browser
  binaries were cleaned through their tooling; current build tooling and the
  installed Edge browser were retained.
- To stop new orders, disable checkout first while preserving order status,
  reconciliation and refund access. Keep native/provider feature flags off until
  their acceptance checks pass.
- Hosting can return to a previous compatible release; Functions can return to
  compatible source. Preserve finalized financial records, refund reservations,
  canonical notifications and published assets. A code rollback does not undo a
  payment, refund, sent notification or publication.
- Editor originals and versioned private drafts remain available for recovery.
  Retain additive fields/indexes until older clients and references are audited.
