## Full change list

### New

- **Paying for an advert.** `startAdPayment` opens a Paystack transaction for
  the total the backend computed and stored; the app opens Paystack's hosted
  page in the browser. `confirmAdPayment` verifies the reference on return —
  automatically on resume, and on demand from a button — and `paystackWebhook`
  settles it independently for anyone who pays and never comes back. All three
  funnel into one idempotent `settlePaidCampaign`.
  - No amount is ever accepted from the phone, and no card or mobile-money
    detail passes through the app or this backend.
  - A short payment is its own state (`underpaid`) rather than silently
    treated as paid.
  - The webhook verifies `x-paystack-signature` against the raw body, in
    constant time, before reading a field.
- **A review desk that covers adverts, off the rail.** The desk is a two-half
  screen — Contributions (unchanged) and Adverts. A reviewer sees the creative,
  the copy, the placements, the regions and what was actually paid, and can
  approve, reject with a reason, pause or resume. Backed by `decideAdCampaign`,
  which is role-gated to validators and above and refuses to approve a campaign
  that has not been paid for.
  - It is **no longer a sixth nav tab**. The rail carries the same five content
    destinations for everybody — a rail that grows an item for staff changes
    the shape of the one strip of the app everybody shares, and moves every
    other destination under a different thumb position. The desk is reached
    from a card at the top of Contribute, which is where the work it reviews is
    sent from, and the card carries a count of what is waiting.
  - Non-reviewers see no card at all — not a locked one. A door somebody can
    never open is worse than no door.
  - Approving starts the run *then*, not at payment, so a campaign held up in
    review does not lose the days it spent waiting.
  - The Adverts tab carries a count of what is waiting.
  - `francis@pwavwe.com` seeded as `reviewer` via the new
    `firebase/seed/set-role.mjs` bootstrap script.
- **Ad notices reach people.** The `notifications` fan-out gained a `push`
  channel alongside email and SMS, on a new `indigen_account` notification
  channel ("Your work and payments") so muting community activity does not mute
  the outcome of an advert somebody paid for. Payment receipts and review
  decisions go out as in-app + email + push.
- **Explore search.** A search screen reached from the header: Top / Reels /
  People, a grid of matching reels that opens as a feed of its own, recent
  searches kept in `SharedPreferences` (local, never synced) and suggestions
  drawn from the feed's own eyebrows. Reels are matched on the device against
  the feed already in memory, so results appear as you type and keep working
  on a stalled connection.
- **For you / Following on Explore**, narrowing the same two sources to the
  people you follow. An empty Following feed says so and offers the way out.
- **Infinite scroll on Explore.** The feed pages by *widening its window*
  rather than carrying a cursor: both sources are live Firestore queries, and a
  cursor would mean stitching pages that can each change underneath the
  stitching. Grows 30 at a time, three reels ahead of the end, to a ceiling of
  300, and resets when the tab is left.
- **Photo cropping.** A `dart:ui`-based cropper (no platform plugin, nothing
  added to a manifest): pan, pinch-zoom anchored on the focal point, quarter
  turns, and Original / 1:1 / 4:5 / 16:9 frames over a rule-of-thirds grid.
  Exports JPEG at the picker's own 1920px ceiling, encoded off the UI isolate.
- **`/ads/payment-complete` on the website.** Where Paystack returns an
  advertiser after checkout. Deliberately makes no claim about whether the
  payment succeeded — a page that read `?status=success` would be one anybody
  could forge — it shows the reference, says to go back to the app, and explains
  what happens if nothing changed.

### Changed

- **Explore videos fit rather than fill.** A reel is somebody's framing, and
  cropping a landscape clip to a portrait screen throws away the sides of the
  shot. The poster stays underneath and fills the letterbox.
- **Explore chrome is quieter.** The wordmark, the LIVE/PREVIEW pill and the
  "3 / 40" position counter are gone. In their place: a search button, the feed
  switch, and a scrubbable progress bar for the clip actually playing. A clip
  that has not opened yet shows a spinner instead of a play button that did
  nothing.
- **Learn and Collection headers stopped being cards.** Both were filled panels
  the eye read as a different screen, sitting on top of the content rather than
  introducing it. Both now sit on the same ground as the rest of the tab, and
  Collection uses the shared `BrandHeader`.
- **Learn opens on the path.** Today's quest and Your momentum are header
  buttons that open a card, each carrying its own figure (`2/3`, `18%`).
- **The composer gets out of the way.** The Community feed's compose button
  slides off as you read further in and returns when you turn back, past a 12px
  threshold so a settling thumb cannot flicker it.
- **Contribute** drops the "Add to the living collection" slogan; the heading is
  just "Contribute".
- **Collection** drops the Video portal (Explore is already that feed, with its
  own tab) and the "Published with permission" plate under the grid — every
  piece carries its own licence line where a reader is actually asking.

### Fixed

- **Kawuri was white on white.** Answers sat on a near-white card while the
  screen is always the night theme, so the palette ink inside them resolved to
  near-white as well. Both speakers now share the heritage-green ground.
- **Reels showed 0 likes and 0 comments under a filled heart and a live
  conversation.** The three totals shared one `Future.wait`, and one is always
  refused: `reelViews` is readable only by the viewer who wrote the row, so an
  aggregate over a whole reel is a query the rules cannot approve — and that
  refusal took the appreciations and the replies down with it. Each total is now
  counted in its own request.
- **Profile pages kept a whitish patch in dark mode.** The backdrop's lit corner
  was a literal cream; it is now `BrandGradients.pageWash`, lifted off the
  palette's own ground. Same for the lesson screen, and both screens' system
  bars follow the theme.

## Known gaps in this build

- **Paystack is on TEST keys.** Payments complete end to end but no money moves;
  the payment panel says so on screen. Swap
  `firebase functions:secrets:set PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` and `PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY`
  for live keys before charging anyone.
- **The Paystack webhook is not registered yet.** Until it is, a campaign is
  settled only by the app's own confirm call — which covers anyone who returns
  to the app, and nobody who does not.
- **Adverts are reviewed but not yet served.** `decideAdCampaign` moves a
  campaign to ACTIVE and sets its run window; nothing renders an ACTIVE campaign
  into the Community, Explore or Collection placements yet, and
  `metrics.impressions` / `metrics.clicks` stay at zero.
- **Reels have no public view count.** Left deliberately: making one would mean
  publishing who watched what. If it is wanted, it belongs in a counter a
  trigger maintains, not a widened read rule.
- **2.9 MB of unused assets still ship.** `assets/characters/kawuri/` is
  declared in `pubspec.yaml` but no Dart code loads it.
