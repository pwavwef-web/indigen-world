One theme, arriving in four places at once: a thing should say what it is.

A link pasted into a post used to be a grey chip with a raw URL on it, which
told a reader nothing and asked them to tap and find out. It now carries the
page's own picture, headline and sentence. An advert used to say "Sponsored"
and nothing else; it can now be asked why it is there, and it answers. The
subscription used to be called "Support this work", which asks for charity for
something that gives back; it is called Membership. And a profile now says
which parts of itself are still empty.

The largest piece of engineering here is the first one, and almost none of it
is on the phone.

## Full change list

### New - a link that says what it is

- **A shared link unfurls into a card.** `CommunityLinkPreview` was a grey chip
  with the host on one line and the raw URL on the other. It now draws the
  `og:image` at `pictureAspect = 1.91` - the ratio Open Graph asks publishers
  for - over the host in capitals, the title at two lines and the description at
  two. A page that gives nothing usable stays exactly the chip it was.

- **One fetch for the whole community, not one per reader.** `fetchLinkPreview`
  reads the page once on the backend and writes a `linkPreviews` document keyed
  by the sha-256 of the normalised URL. Every phone reads that document itself
  first, so a link somebody has already shared costs one document read and no
  function invocation at all. The member's own IP never reaches the page being
  unfurled.

- **The card names the link you are about to open, never where it redirected
  to.** The host over the headline comes from the address actually typed into
  the post, not from the final hop. The two differ exactly when a link
  redirects, and naming the destination would let somebody post `evil.test`,
  redirect it to a newspaper, and have the feed draw that newspaper's name,
  headline and photograph over a link that opens `evil.test`. The stated cost
  is that a shortened link is honestly labelled with the shortener.

- **One link is one document however it was shared.** `normaliseLinkUrl` exists
  twice, in TypeScript and in Dart, both building the key from parts rather than
  from a URL printer, because the two printers disagree. Both drop the fragment,
  any `utm_*` parameter and the same fourteen tracking names (`fbclid`,
  `gclid`, `igshid`, `twclid` and the rest). So one article shared twenty ways
  is one document and one fetch - and the cache key never carries anybody's
  click id. Two pinned sha-256 vectors in the Dart test are the contract the
  TypeScript side owes.

- **The unfurler is not allowed to fetch the inside of the network.**
  `assertPublicHost` runs at *every* hop of the redirect chain rather than once
  on the pasted link, refuses `localhost`, `*.localhost` and `*.internal` by
  name, and refuses an address if *any* answer in a multi-A record is private.
  It covers both families: 10/8, 127/8, 169.254/16 (named in the comment as the
  metadata service), 172.16-31, 192.168/16, CGNAT and multicast on the v4 side,
  and `fc00::/7`, `fe80::/10`, 6to4 and NAT64 on the v6 side, with a hand-rolled
  `expandIPv6` so the checks run on numbers rather than on text.

- **Bounded everywhere a stranger's page could push.** 512 KB of body read
  through a manual reader that cancels the stream in a `finally`; 8 s a hop and
  20 s for the whole chain, deliberately under the callable's own 30 s so a slow
  chain fails with time left to record the failure; at most 4 redirects; and
  `</head>` or 128 KB, whichever comes first, before any parser sees the
  document. The meta and link regexes are `[^>]{0,2048}` rather than `[^>]*`,
  and the title is found with four plain `indexOf` scans rather than a lazy
  backtracking group, because whoever posts the link chooses the page.

- **The bot says who it is.** `IndigenWorldBot/1.0` with a contact URL,
  explicitly not disguised as a browser.

- **The cache is world-readable and client-unwritable.** The new
  `match /linkPreviews/{previewId}` block is `allow read: if true; allow write:
  if false`. Read is open because a guest reading the feed would otherwise see a
  bare URL where everybody else saw an article, and the card is metadata about a
  URL already sitting in world-readable `communityPosts` text. Nothing but the
  Admin SDK writes, because a client that could write here could put any picture
  and any headline under any link in the community, which is a near-perfect
  phishing surface.

- **A post that brought its own photograph keeps it.** The preview is guarded on
  `!post.hasMedia`, so an attachment and a link card never compete for the same
  eye. The link stays tappable in the post text above.

### New - an advert that says why it is there

- **"Why am I seeing this?"** Every `SponsoredCard` header now carries a 16px
  info button opening a popup that states that the content is free, that
  adverts pay for it, that nobody is tracked, profiled or targeted, and that a
  membership removes every advert. The claim about targeting matches the serving
  code rather than being aspirational: `adsForPlacement` filters on active,
  headline, placement and campaign window only, and `ServedAd.regions` is never
  read on the serving path, even though the advertiser wizard collects it.

- **Adverts run through the Collection channels.** `collectionRowsWithAds`
  splices at `kCollectionListAdCadence = 5` into six list surfaces - the
  dictionary, published channels, the shop, the apps directory, heroes, and the
  flat music/audiobook list. Before this, the Collection placement reached
  exactly one tile at the bottom of the grid. No new `AdPlacement` was added, so
  campaigns already sold "Collection" now serve across seven surfaces rather
  than the single tile they were bought against - **worth saying plainly to
  advertisers**, since it enlarges what the placement means without moving
  anyone outside what they paid for.

- **Never in a search result.** Both searchable Collection surfaces splice only
  while the query is empty, and the grid tile keeps its existing rule. Somebody
  who has typed a word never has a paid card placed between them and the thing
  they named.

- **Adverts line up with the list they sit in.** `SponsoredCard` gained a
  `margin` parameter; every channel with its own gutter passes `EdgeInsets.zero`
  so the paid row is exactly as wide as the rows above and below it.

### New - Collection, Explore and the paywall

- **Published channels gained search and pull-to-refresh.** A search field
  appears once a channel holds at least six items, with per-kind hints, and the
  screen gained a `RefreshIndicator`. The refresh helper invalidates and then
  awaits the replacement data, so the spinner comes down when the data has
  actually landed.

- **A quieter Collection tab.** "The Kassena Collection" at headlineLarge w900
  over a slogan became a single ellipsised line, "Kasem Collections", at the
  size the Community header uses. Portal tiles lost their radial-gradient wash
  and their colour tint; the channel colour survives only as a rounded plate
  behind the icon, and tiles shrank with the artwork (minHeight 156 to 128).

- **Explore deals creators in turn.** `variedByCreator` buckets reels by author
  and deals round-robin, so one person's uploads no longer run back to back in
  publish order. Each creator's own reels are shuffled with a seed derived from
  a hand-rolled FNV-1a over the creator id - deliberately not `Object.hashCode`,
  which Dart salts per process - so the arrangement is identical on every launch
  and cannot move when a *different* creator posts. That stability is the point:
  the feed is rebuilt whenever either snapshot ticks, and a plain shuffle would
  teleport the pager mid-swipe.

- **The paywall is called Membership.** The heading is now "The archive is free.
  Membership keeps it running.", and the body says what the money buys: the
  servers, the recording trips, and the people who check each contribution. The
  small print gained a promise - "Nothing in the archive is ever locked behind a
  membership, and nothing you have contributed depends on one."

- **A "Save N%" badge computed from Play's own prices.** `yearlySavingsPercent`
  compares the monthly offer's `rawPrice * 12` against the yearly one. No
  discount is written down anywhere in the app, which is the same rule the file
  already applies to prices. Yearly is the preselected plan, so this is what a
  member sees on a cold open.

### New - one place for your profile, one page for your alerts

- **The Profile tab.** The second destination is renamed from "Community" to
  "Profile" and is now where a member's identity is *managed*: an identity card
  with separate **Edit profile** and **Preview** buttons, and a completeness
  card with a progress bar and up to three "add this" chips. `Preview` opens the
  member's own public page, because what somebody wants to know before they post
  is what a stranger sees, not what their own form looks like.

- **Your own public page no longer edits itself.** The `Edit profile` button on
  your own community profile became a non-interactive "This is your page" pill.
  `EditCommunityProfileScreen` now has exactly one push site in the whole app.

- **A Notification settings page.** The nine preference switches left the
  Settings list for a page of their own, split into "ON THIS DEVICE" and "WHAT
  WAKES YOU" - a real split, not a cosmetic one: the device pair is stored on
  the handset and also rewrites this device's push registration, while the nine
  live on the account, so silencing a handset does not silence a tablet. A
  closing paragraph says exactly that, and adds that muting everything does not
  empty the in-app notifications list.

- **One row mutes everything.** "Mute everything" sends a single Firestore
  `update()` carrying an explicit field path for all nine values - one write,
  not a loop of nine that could half-succeed - and it enumerates the known keys
  rather than replacing the map, so anything ever stored beside them survives.

### Changed

- **Post attachments moved back into the avatar gutter.** Media had been lifted
  out of the text column to span the card; it now sits in the same left-inset
  column as the text, poll and action bar, so the avatar gutter is a straight
  line the whole height of every post.

- **The community profile can no longer be edited from Settings**, and the
  Profile screen's Settings tab lost its community-profile row. Both were second
  doors to a thing that now has one.

- **The bell's settings icon opens the new page** instead of a two-button popup
  that only offered push on or off.

### Fixed

- **A skipped word scrolls back to the question.** The two skip buttons sit
  below Send, so on a real phone a member has to scroll down to reach them.
  Tapping one swapped the word - which arrives at the top - without moving the
  viewport, so it read as nothing having happened. Skip now runs the same 240 ms
  scroll back to the top that Send has always run, and drops the keyboard the
  same way. The scroll deliberately does not wait on the network: the controller
  advances its buffer before its first `await`, so the next word is already in
  hand when the animation starts.

## Deliberately not done

- **DNS rebinding is not defended against, and the code says so.** The doc
  comment on `assertPublicHost` states outright that a name resolving publicly
  at check time and privately a moment later is not covered, that closing it
  properly means pinning the resolved address into the connection, and that the
  platform's `fetch` does not expose that. The residual risk is named precisely
  - one unauthenticated GET whose response never reaches the person who asked
  for it - rather than left for a reader to discover.
- **No oEmbed, no per-site special cases, no favicon, no image proxy.** One GET
  and a `<head>` read, the same for every site. The `og:image` is loaded by the
  handset directly, so the reader's IP does reach the image host even though it
  never reaches the page - what the server does instead is refuse to hand the
  phone a dangerous target. Note the image guard is narrower than the page
  guard: it rejects non-http(s), `localhost` and private IP *literals*, but does
  no DNS lookup, so a hostname that resolves privately is not caught there.
- **robots.txt is neither fetched nor parsed.** The honest User-Agent is what
  makes a refusal possible; a site that wants to say no must do it at the edge.
  The promise is that the bot will not retry from a different disguise, not that
  it reads the file.
- **`canonicalUrl` is stored and never read.** Recorded on every document for
  later; nothing on the phone looks at it yet.
- **Only the first link in a post, and only in the main post card.** Quoted
  posts and the composer draw no card.
- **Completeness is a nudge, never a gate.** Nothing keys off the score, there
  is no red state and no reward for finishing - "a member who wants to be a grey
  circle called Amina is allowed to be one". Only the first three missing items
  are shown, because six grey chips is a list of failures. One caveat worth
  recording: a display name is validated non-empty at both creation and edit, so
  the bar's real range is 1/6 to 6/6 and the "A name" chip can never appear.
- **"Mute everything" is a row, not a tenth switch**, because it has no state of
  its own and a switch dressed as one can end up reading "on" because a single
  row disagreed with it.
- **Nothing on the notifications page is optimistic.** Both writes await the
  repository and let the Firestore listener move the switches, so a refused
  write snaps a switch back rather than leaving it looking saved.
- **Explore's two halves are not interleaved.** Variety is applied inside the
  published half and inside the community half, never across the concatenation,
  because interleaving would put a clip filmed this morning ahead of the archive
  the tab exists to show. The consequence is that the "no two in a row" property
  holds within each half and not at the seam between them.
- **The savings badge refuses five ways** - a missing plan, mismatched
  currencies, a non-positive price, a yearly plan that is not actually cheaper,
  and any saving under 5% - because a "Save 20%" typed into the file is a claim
  the app cannot keep once Play's regional pricing moves.
- **Play Billing itself was not touched.** Despite the copy rewrite, `_buy`,
  `_restore`, the purchase listener, the integrity gate and every
  `PurchaseOutcome` string are unchanged. There is no new billing behaviour in
  this release.

### Where the coverage is thinner than the change

Recorded because the two unchanged counts above are easy to read as "nothing
needed testing":

- **`services/functions/src/link-preview.ts` has no tests at all** - not the
  SSRF address checks, not `expandIPv6`, not the bounded parsers, not the
  redirect and budget behaviour, and not the TypeScript half of the cache-key
  contract that the Dart test pins from one side. This is not a missing harness:
  `firebase/tests/` already runs eleven compiled backend modules under
  `test:function-helpers` by exactly the convention this file's exports follow.
  The runner is set up; the suite was simply not written.
- **The `linkPreviews` rule has no rules test**, and it is the only
  unauthenticated-read grant added in this release. The write lock is what
  stands between the community and the phishing surface the rules file itself
  describes.
- The notification-preferences test asserts that each preference's *title* is on
  screen, not that a switch exists - the `reason` string says "has no switch"
  but the matcher is `find.text`. Only one of the nine ever reaches a real
  `SwitchListTile`. Nothing asserts the write path at all, because the harness
  leaves the repository null and every control disabled.
- `_ProfileCompleteness`, the Edit/Preview pair, and every signed-in branch of
  the Profile tab are uncovered - `profile_auth_test.dart` runs entirely as a
  guest.

## Known, unfixed, and worth a decision

- **`www.indigenworld.com` does not resolve.** The Android manifest declares it
  alongside the apex as an `autoVerify` App Links host, but the name has no DNS
  record at all (`NXDOMAIN`), while `indigenworld.com` resolves to
  `199.36.158.100` and serves correctly. Either add the record and the Firebase
  Hosting custom domain, or drop the `www` line from the intent filter. Leaving
  a declared host unreachable means Android records a verification failure
  against it.
- **`apps/website/config/app-links.json` is stale and inert.** It carries an
  empty fingerprint list and names the package `world.indigen.mobile`, which is
  the Gradle namespace and the *non-production* application id - production is
  `com.indigenworld.indigen`. The build therefore skips generating
  `.well-known/assetlinks.json` entirely. This is currently harmless, because
  the **live file is correct and complete** (see below) and Hosting preserves
  it. But if anyone ever regenerates from this config, it would overwrite a good
  file with one naming the wrong package. Fix the config to match what is live
  before touching that path.
- **The Collection cadence comment is wrong.** `kCollectionListAdCadence = 5` is
  documented as "looser than the Community timeline's and looser than Explore's"
  - but `kCommunityAdCadence` is 10 and `kExploreAdCadence` is 6, so per row a
  Collection channel is the **densest** of the three. The number may well be
  right; the sentence justifying it is not.
- **"Why am I seeing this?" is not on every advert.** It lives on
  `SponsoredCard`, which is the Community timeline plus the six new Collection
  channels. The Collection grid's `SponsoredTile` does not have it, and Explore's
  adverts are not `SponsoredCard`s at all - they render as full-screen reels -
  so they have no info icon, no popup and no route to Membership. Advert
  transparency is currently inconsistent across the three placements.
- **The new pull-to-refresh is inert where a short channel would be pulled.**
  `PublishedCollectionScreen` wraps its `CustomScrollView` in a
  `RefreshIndicator` but never sets `AlwaysScrollableScrollPhysics` - there is no
  `physics:` in the file - so a channel whose contents fit the viewport does not
  overscroll and the gesture never fires.
- **The word-queue skip buttons stay live through their own 240 ms scroll**, so
  a second tap during the animation skips a second word.
- Three of the four `PublishedCollectionScreen` wrappers (`Music`, `Audiobook`,
  `Video`) are referenced nowhere outside their own declarations - the Music and
  Audiobook portals open `MusicScreen`, and Video has no portal. This change
  extends dead code along with the live path.
