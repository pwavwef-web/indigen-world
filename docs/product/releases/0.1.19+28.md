---

0.1.18 was about work still being there when somebody came back for it. This
release is about what that work **means**. A reel now arrives with a topic, an
account of what is happening in it, the name of whoever made it and the
contributor's declaration of their right to share it — and Explore shows all of
that in its Context panel instead of leaving a video to explain itself.

The same idea runs through the rest of the release. Explore became a proper
cultural feed with topics, framing that respects the shape of what was filmed,
and a Context panel that says where every piece of information came from.
Communities became real places people can join, run and keep private. And
Kawuri remembers conversations, labels what it knows from the phone, and never
passes a guess off as a Kasem translation.

## Full change list

### New - making a reel is three steps, and every reel explains itself

- **Media.** Record or choose a video, then trim its start and end on a
  thumbnail timeline with a playhead, pick a cover (a frame, your own picture,
  or one chosen automatically), keep or remove the original sound, and add
  captions. The current length and the 3:00 limit are always on screen.
- **Landscape and square clips are previewed exactly as Explore will show
  them**, using Explore's own framing code, and can be dragged to keep the
  important part in view, with a warning when a lot will be cropped.
- **Captions** can be uploaded (SRT, VTT, or a plain transcript whose timing is
  estimated and says so) or typed and timed against the video, in Kasem,
  English, French, Twi, Hausa or another language. They are only published once
  the creator confirms they match what is said. There is no automatic caption
  generation: the project has no transcription service.
- **Tell the story.** Under "Give this reel meaning": a caption of up to 500
  characters with line breaks kept, a topic (Storytelling, Music, Dance,
  Traditions, Food, Clothing, Craft, History, Community life, Other), a
  community chosen from those the member has joined, "What is happening?" for
  Explore's Context panel, who made it (pre-filled, or someone else and their
  source organisation), and a required rights declaration.
- **Review.** An Explore-accurate preview, a summary of every choice with a way
  back to change it, and Publish.
- **Drafts stay on the phone.** Text saves itself as it is typed; Save draft,
  resume, delete with confirmation and the last-edited time are all there. The
  video is moved into the draft once and never re-uploaded as text changes.
- **Publishing shows what is really happening:** preparing, uploading with real
  byte progress, pause and resume, failure in words, retry, processing and
  published. A retry reuses a video that already uploaded, and a publish that
  landed without the phone hearing back is found rather than repeated, so a reel
  cannot be posted twice. Leaving mid-upload offers Continue upload, Save and
  leave, or Cancel upload.
- **Trims, sound and captions are honoured in Explore, the community feed and
  the full-screen viewer.** Explore's Context panel shows the creator's account
  of what is happening, their stated source and the rights declaration.

**What this does not do yet.** The app has no video transcoder, so a trim
chooses what plays and removing the sound mutes playback; the whole recording
is uploaded and nothing is compressed on the phone. Older app versions play the
full file with its sound. A server-side render (trim, compress, strip sound,
shift caption timings) is the backend work that finishes this, and filters,
overlays, music and multi-clip editing wait on it.

### New - Explore is a cultural feed

- **Topics:** For you, Music, Stories and Traditions, read from the Collection
  channel, post category, category text, tags and captions.
- **One ranked list** balancing recency, follows, joined communities, review
  status, context and topic interest, with a diversity pass so no single creator,
  community or language takes over.
- **Pictures from the published archive** join video; community posts still
  reach Explore only when they carry a video.
- **Framing that respects the shot:** portrait fills the screen towards its focal
  point, landscape and square are enlarged only as far as is safe and otherwise
  framed over a soft backdrop made from the media.
- **The chrome gets out of the way** during playback and comes back on tap,
  pause, a new reel or when a sheet opens; it is always visible to screen
  readers.
- **The Context sheet** has three heights and labels every source separately:
  creator-provided, review desk, community-reviewed dictionary, and AI-generated
  (never presented as fact).
- **Community reels carry a Join button**, views are counted only after a
  qualified view, and mute is remembered.

### New - communities

- **Find, join or start a community**, public or private, from the new
  Communities tab: search by name, language, place or topic.
- **Private communities are private in the rules**, not just the interface. Their
  posts and media are readable only by active members; a request to join waits
  for a moderator.
- **Owners and admins** edit the name, description, category, language, place,
  pictures and rules; approve and decline requests; appoint moderators; hand
  the community over; or close it when they are the last member.
- **Community links** (`indigenworld.com/communities/<slug>`) open the community
  in the app. The matching website page is not deployed yet - see Deployment.

### New - the community feed invites people in

- **"Make a post"** sits at the top of the feed with shortcuts to add a photo or
  a video, and a post can be filed as a Question, Language, Culture, Music or
  Story post (Announcement is reserved for staff and community moderators).
- **"Today in Kasem"** is a one-line prompt to share a word from home.
- **"New voices"** suggests people worth following between posts: new members,
  people near you, people in your communities, creators and people posting now.
- The feed says **"You're all caught up"** when it has shown everything.

### Changed - Kawuri

- **Conversations are kept, per account**, with search and deletion that asks
  first. A reply still arrives in the conversation it was asked in, even if
  another chat was opened while it was pending, and an interrupted request or a
  half-written question recovers after the app is reopened.
- **A welcome with starters** replaces the empty box; a suggestion fills an
  editable draft instead of sending itself.
- **Answers from the on-device guide are labelled as such**, Kawuri says plainly
  that it can be wrong about the language, and lists and numbered steps render
  properly instead of as raw markdown.
- **Translations come only from exact published dictionary matches**; a fuzzy
  suggestion is never presented as a verified translation.
- **Contributions drafted with Kawuri** carry an AI-assistance disclosure for the
  reviewer.

### Changed - Membership is the plans

- The Membership tab in My Space now opens straight on the plans: Plus, Patron
  and Creator over a swipeable card carousel, Monthly and Yearly side by side
  with the saving shown, and Yearly selected by default. A member's status and
  the Google Play Manage link sit in a card above the plans.
