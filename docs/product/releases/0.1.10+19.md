The release that turns contributing from one long form into a place a member
can return to. There is a guided queue of words to translate, a server-owned
contributors board, points and streaks for accepted work, a route for asking
the project to add a Kassena name it does not know yet, and switches for every
kind of community alert that can wake a phone.

## Full change list

### New

- **A guided word queue.** Contribute now hands a member a real word with the
  example sentence that gives it context, asks for its Kasem rendering, dialect
  and part of speech, and keeps the next words close enough that answering does
  not become a wait between forms.
  - More than one rendering can be entered with commas, slashes or line breaks;
    the chips under the field show exactly what will be stored.
  - “I don't know this one” and “I'm not sure from this sentence” both move on
    without judging the member or inviting a guess. Answered and skipped words
    do not come back to that member.
  - An answer becomes an ordinary Collection contribution and goes through the
    existing review desk. It does not publish itself. Approval closes the word;
    rejection or withdrawal makes it available again when no accepted answer
    remains.
  - Tatoeba example sentences carry their sentence id, contributor and CC BY
    2.0 FR licence from the queue into the review and publication shapes. An
    unattributed sentence invents no credit.

- **Contribution points, streaks and a public contributors board.** Accepted
  work is scored by the backend, never by the phone: 10 points for a dictionary
  word, 25 for a written piece, and 50 for a recording or film. Each award is
  tied to the contribution id, so a retried Firestore event cannot pay twice.
  - The Contribute hub shows the leading contributors and opens a ranked board.
    A member outside the visible leaders keeps their own position pinned at the
    bottom rather than disappearing from the screen.
  - Learn's XP badge now adds accepted contribution points to learning XP, and
    the detail view says how much came from each half. The server-owned score is
    kept separate from the offline, owner-writable learning record.
  - Consecutive contribution days build a streak. At 18:00 Ghana time, a streak
    of two days or more that will lapse that night may receive one reminder.
    Members can switch that reminder off.
  - Passing somebody on the board can notify at most three people from the
    visible ranking window; ties are not treated as an overtake.

- **Requests for missing Kassena names.** A real name absent from the published
  list is now an offer to ask, not a dead-end validation message. The request
  records the name, its kind, an optional meaning and a note for the validator,
  while showing the ASCII fold a community handle can actually carry.
  - The validator desk has a name-request queue with approve and reject paths.
  - Approval publishes the name and, when the request came from a handle claim,
    hands over that handle in the same decision. The member receives the result
    in their notification centre and by SMS when a phone number is available.

- **Per-topic notification controls.** Settings now carries switches for posts
  from followed people, replies in joined or saved threads, Explore comments,
  mentions, likes, follows, like milestones, contributor-board changes and
  streak reminders. Only an explicit switch-off mutes a topic, so existing
  profiles continue to behave as they did before this screen existed.

### Changed

- **Contribute is a hub instead of a form.** The tab opens on the people doing
  the work, then offers two clear doors: translate queued words, or add something
  to the archive. “My submissions” is its own screen, and a completed form ends
  on a receipt with a direct route there.
  - The archive chooser names the act — add a word, saying, song or video —
    rather than asking a member to understand the Collection's internal shelf
    taxonomy first.
  - Audiobooks are deliberately absent from the phone chooser. A narration has
    a rights holder, a narrator and hours of media, so it is curated by the
    project team in the admin console; the audiobook shelf now says that plainly.
  - The reviewers' door remains first for accounts that have one, and remains
    absent rather than locked for everybody else.

- **Dictionary entries can carry the whole answer.** Lists show the first
  rendering or meaning and an honest count of the rest; the entry screen shows
  them all, including example-sentence attribution. Search matches meanings
  and renderings that were not first in the row, and screen-reader labels read
  the complete entry rather than the shortened visual summary.

- **Explore no longer ends at the end of a young archive.** Once there is
  nothing further to fetch, a feed of at least three live reels is queued again
  in a deterministic new order. The seam never repeats the same reel twice,
  views and advert impressions remain once per real item, paid placements are
  exhausted rather than multiplied, and leaving Explore resets the loop.

- **Downloads are reachable from Collection.** Subscribers get a downloads
  action beside the profile orb, with a badge for what is actually stored on the
  phone. The heading reserves the space on the first frame so entitlement
  loading does not make it jump sideways.

### Fixed

- **Google Sign-In no longer mistakes every Android `canceled` result for a
  deliberate dismissal.** A real swipe-away still closes quietly. A suppressed,
  interrupted or misconfigured native account sheet now falls through to the
  hosted Firebase route, and a failure shown to the member includes the provider
  detail needed to distinguish certificate, client and device problems.
- **A failed word top-up no longer interrupts the word already on screen.** The
  current answer stays in place, a failed submission keeps the typed text, and
  a stalled queue stops asking instead of spinning against the rate limiter.
