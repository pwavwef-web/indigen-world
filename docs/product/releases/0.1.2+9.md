## Full change list

### New
- **Adverts.** A six-step campaign builder with creative picker, region
  targeting (Navrongo, Paga, Chiana, Upper East, Northern Ghana, All of Ghana)
  and a running price preview. Lives as a profile tab and standalone at
  `/ads`, where an advertising notification lands.
- **Validation desk.** Role-gated review queue (Waiting / Approved /
  Escalated / Published) with a submission review screen. Only mounted for
  accounts whose `role` claim Security Rules and `decideSubmission` accept.
- **Post a reel from Explore.** Publishes as an ordinary community post, so
  it inherits the existing likes, replies, block/mute/report and deletion
  paths. Prompts for sign-in and a community handle before the recorder.
- **Saved words** and **Your contributions** screens on the profile; the
  Approved stat is now counted rather than stubbed at zero.
- **Video / film** collection kind, including `/collection/video` deep links.

### Changed
- Glass material system (`lib/shared/glass_surface.dart`) applied app-wide:
  cards and inputs default to translucent panes with a lit hairline edge in
  place of white boxes with grey outlines.
- Contribute type picker sized by `mainAxisExtent`, removing the dead band
  under the picker on tablets.
- Settings rows carry subtitles.
- Community post card, people widgets and collection surfaces reworked onto
  the glass surfaces.

### Backend
- `services/functions/src/ads.ts` and Firestore rules + tests for ad campaigns.

## Known gaps in this build
- **Adverts cannot be paid for.** Paystack is not wired; the final step
  records what is owed and parks the campaign as awaiting payment. Anyone
  completing the flow reaches a dead end.
- **2.9 MB of unused assets ship in the bundle.** `assets/characters/kawuri/`
  (spritesheet, avatar, celebrate frames) is declared in `pubspec.yaml` but
  no Dart code loads it.
- AGP 8.13.1 — Flutter will drop support below 9.0.1 soon.
