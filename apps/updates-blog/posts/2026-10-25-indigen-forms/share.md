New on Indigen World: Indigen Forms. The team can now build, preview and publish contribution forms (Kasem words, translations, oral histories, research and feedback) straight from Administration, without waiting for an app release. Contributors find them under Available forms in the contributor portal, save drafts, check their answers before sending and get a reference for every response. Reviewers decide with clear checks and a full history, and every permission stays a separate choice that approval never overrides.

Available in Administration and the contributor portal from 25 October 2026 for signed-in contributors.
