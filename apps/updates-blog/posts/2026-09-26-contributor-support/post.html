Getting stuck at sign-in should not leave a contributor without a way to ask for help. We have prepared a new support page for account and workspace concerns.

Help before you sign in

The support page covers sign-in, missing reset emails, activation, assignments, saving work and payment concerns. Enter your name, the email from your invitation and a short description. Automatic guidance gives you a starting point, and you can ask a person to take over.

A case you can return to

Keep the private link to your case. You can return to read replies, add information and confirm when the problem is solved. Anyone with this link can read and reply to the case, so keep it private. Never send passwords, reset links, verification codes or payment details in a message.

Password recovery is sent only to the registered mailbox of an eligible contributor account. Support messages do not change passwords or approve payments.

A clearer view for the team

The admin support inbox brings these cases together. Staff can reply, assign a case to themselves and record how a resolution was confirmed. Unresolved cases produce internal reminders. Sending instructions alone does not close a case.

Availability

Prepared in the repository; public deployment is not yet confirmed. After release, choose “Need help signing in?” on the contributor sign-in screen. Existing assignment reports remain available.

The WhatsApp connection is prepared but is not active. It still requires number registration, account verification and a successful live messaging check. There is no guaranteed response time. Email replies to the team mailbox are handled there; they are not automatically added to a support case.
