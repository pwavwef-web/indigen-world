Contributors can earn points for expressions approved by the Review Desk. Each approved expression earns 10 points, up to 300 points for approvals in one UTC day. A submission awaiting review does not earn points yet, and revisions of the same expression do not earn another award.

The Points tab beside Expressions shows available points and the reward rate. Once a contributor has at least 300 points, they can choose any whole-point amount from 300 up to their available balance and request airtime or data for a Ghana mobile number on MTN, Telecel or AT. At the default rate, 300 points equals GH₵5; other amounts are converted proportionally and rounded to the nearest pesewa. The team reviews and fulfils the request in the Admin console; delivery is recorded with a reference. If the request is rejected, the points return to the contributor's balance. Airtime and data are not delivered automatically.

The rewards screen uses the portal's blue and white design. It groups the available balance, points per expression, and daily limit at the top. An Earn section explains how points accumulate; the Redeem section presents airtime and data as separate choices. Contributors can review earlier requests in History.

Administrators can adjust points per expression, the daily cap, the minimum redemption, and the cedi amount in Contributor Payments. These settings apply to future awards and requests.
