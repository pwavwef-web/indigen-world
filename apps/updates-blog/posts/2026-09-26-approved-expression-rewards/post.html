Every carefully considered Kasem expression helps build a record of how people speak. Contributors can now earn points when their work is approved, and use those points to request airtime or mobile data.

AvailabilityPoints and rewards are live for invited contributors with active accounts. Airtime and data requests are reviewed and delivered manually by the team.

Points follow approval

Submit your work for review as usual. When the Review Desk approves an expression, its points appear in your available balance under Points. Work still awaiting review does not earn points yet.

The initial rate is 10 points per approved expression. Awards are subject to the configured daily limit, and administrators can adjust the rate and limit for future approvals. Revising the same expression does not earn a second award.

Your earlier work counts

Eligible earlier submissions that reached Approved or Published status have also been credited. Work that has not yet been approved can earn points when it is approved.

Keep your streak goingYour streak follows daily submissions. You can keep contributing while earlier work moves through review; points follow the approval decision.

Request a reward and follow its progress

Open Points → Redeem to see the available rewards. Once you have enough points, select a reward and enter your delivery details in the pop-up. Check the recipient number before submitting your request.

Open History to follow the request. A pending request is waiting for the team to review it. Approval means the request has been accepted; delivery is a separate step. The team sends the airtime or data manually and marks it delivered afterwards.

If a request is rejected, its reason appears in History and the deducted points are returned to your available balance.

Getting started

Returning contributors can sign in to the contributor workspace and open Points. Use Earn to return to your tasks, Redeem to request a reward, and History to see your requests.

Thank you to the contributors and reviewers helping make everyday Kasem knowledge available to more people.
