## WhatsApp / community announcement

Indigen World 0.1.29 is here.

When the dictionary doesn't know a word, it now asks you. Every so often Explore shows a
word that's waiting for its Kasem, and a search that finds nothing, a topic page or Kawuri
can all take you straight to it. Write your answer, sign in, send — and follow it in Your
submissions while a reviewer checks it.

You choose whether you're credited by name, and whether your answer may help train
language tools (that one is off unless you turn it on).

New too: Practise with Kawuri — short lessons from verified dictionary words.

Update Indigen World from Google Play. It may take a day or two to reach your phone.
