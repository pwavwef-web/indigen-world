Contributors can work through a manageable first set of 15 tasks, then choose another 15 after submitting that set. This update also remembers your place and makes review progress easier to follow.

AvailabilityImplemented and checked locally; deployment is pending. Daily batches require tasks prepared by the admin. Existing assignments are preserved.

15 first, then another 15 on request

The admin prepares 30 tasks for a contributor and a UTC date. The first 15 become available that day. After all 15 are submitted, the contributor can select Request 15 more tasks to unlock the remaining 15 automatically.

The extra request is available once per UTC day. Repeated clicks do not create more tasks. Drafts and skipped tasks do not count as submitted. Approval is not required to unlock the second batch; points still follow approval.

When both batches have been submitted, the portal says you are done with today’s batch and directs you to review updates. Another day’s tasks depend on the team preparing a new batch. Unrequested extra tasks are available only on their prepared date.

Return to where you left off

Task and contribution lists remember filters, searches and scroll position in the current browser tab. Opening an expression and returning to the list keeps your place. This memory is separate for each contributor account; it does not store the draft text.

Know when your work was submitted

Submitted expressions show their submission date and latest review date when those records are available. Work waiting for review is labelled clearly. There is no estimated review deadline.

Getting started

After deployment, open Tasks in the contributor workspace. The daily task card shows your first-batch progress and when the extra request is available.
