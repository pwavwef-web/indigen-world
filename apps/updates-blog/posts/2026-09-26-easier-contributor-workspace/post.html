Finding your next task, reading feedback and getting help should take less effort. This contributor workspace update brings everyday actions closer, especially on a phone.

AvailabilityPrepared for active invited contributors. These changes are implemented and checked locally; production deployment has not yet been confirmed.

Your next task comes first

Home puts the current task set and its Start, Continue or Revise action near the top. Returned work keeps priority. Updates sit in a compact panel, and attention notices expand when you need them.

A small streak indicator beside the Home greeting shows your current streak and whether you have contributed today. Open it for the full streak view. Streaks follow new daily submissions; points follow approval.

A clearer route through the workspace

The phone navigation now has Home, Tasks, Contributions, Points and More. Help & guide, Activity, Streak, Kawuri and Account remain available through More. Kawuri also stays beside your work in the editor.

More room to write

Task instructions and progress collapse when you open an expression. Feedback stays beside the answer, and alternative translations and usage notes sit in an optional section. Save status, draft recovery and Kasem character buttons remain available.

Find answers and contact the team

Search the guide or expand a topic. Report a problem and My reports are available at the top, with an indicator for team replies. Active contributors can ask about account problems before receiving a task set. Task-specific reports still link to the relevant assignment.

Clearer progress and account settings

Progress names approved work, work awaiting review and revisions separately. Contribution filters put drafts and work needing revision within easy reach. On phones, an account-section selector takes you directly to the selected form.

Payments and pointsBank and MoMo details are for separately arranged payments. Airtime and data point rewards use the recipient number entered in Redeem; they do not require a bank statement.

Getting started

After this update is released, sign in to the contributor workspace and open Home. New contributors also see a short checklist until they dismiss it or submit their first task.
