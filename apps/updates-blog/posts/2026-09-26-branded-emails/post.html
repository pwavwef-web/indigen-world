Messages from Indigen World are getting a consistent look, with familiar indigo, cream, gold and terracotta colours and clearer next steps.

A familiar message from the team

Form acknowledgements, newsletter welcomes, contributor notifications and support replies share a readable layout. The Indigen World name is text, so you can recognise it even when your inbox blocks images. Smaller screens get roomier text and adjusted spacing.

Clearer help with your account

Password-reset emails requested through the support service use the same theme and a clear “Choose a new password” button. A copyable link remains available if a button does not work. Keep recovery links private. If you did not request a reset, you can ignore the message; requesting a reset does not itself change your password.

Invitations with a clear next step

A new validator invitation template explains the review role and invites the recipient to reply so the team can discuss availability and arrange access. Receiving an invitation does not automatically grant account permissions.

Availability

Prepared for release; deployment verification is pending. The shared design covers emails sent by our application. Firebase rejected changes to its separate built-in security templates, so password resets from the sign-in screen and other Firebase account emails still have their original appearance. Email apps may display colours or spacing differently. Replying to a support email reaches the team mailbox; it does not automatically add a reply to your support case.
