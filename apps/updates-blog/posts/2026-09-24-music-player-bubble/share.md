## WhatsApp / community announcement

Indigen World 0.1.23 is out for Android.

Listening to music in the app? Tap the down arrow on the player bar and it folds
into a small bubble that you can drag to either side of the screen, while the
song keeps playing. Tap the bubble to bring the player back. Closing the player
with the X now stops the music and clears it from your notifications.

Sharing a Kasem saying? The contribution form now has optional boxes for what
the words say word for word, when the saying is used, and its French meaning.
Leave a box empty if you are not sure.

Update Indigen World from Google Play. It may take a day or two to reach your
phone.
