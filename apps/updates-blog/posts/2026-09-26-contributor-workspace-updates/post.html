Keep track of your contributions and rewards

We have prepared a few improvements to the contributor portal that make the next step easier to find.

If a submitted task needs revision, a notice in the portal points you back to Tasks. When an airtime or data redemption is marked as delivered, a notice can take you to Points History. You can dismiss a notice after reading it.

After you submit a redemption request, the portal opens History so you can see its pending status. Requests still go through staff review and manual delivery; submitting one does not mean the airtime or data has already been sent.

On a day when you have not yet contributed, the Streak screen offers a Go to Tasks button. The redemption form also keeps keyboard focus inside the popup until you close or submit it, making the form easier to use without a mouse.

These changes are implemented in the repository. We will confirm their availability after deployment and a check with authenticated contributor accounts.
