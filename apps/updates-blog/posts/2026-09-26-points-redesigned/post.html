Your contributions deserve a page with some energy. We have redesigned Points with a bold personal score, a little motion, and more room for the people helping the archive grow.

Preview This redesign is implemented in the app code. An Android release containing it has not yet been confirmed.

Your impact, at a glance

A new score card puts your contribution points up front, alongside your approved contributions and day streak. A gold emblem settles into place as the page opens, and the score counts up to your actual total.

Less reading. Clearer next steps.

Three small tiles show the earning scale: words +10, writing +25, and recordings +50. Points still arrive after review and approval. Tap Contribute to choose what to send, or the information button for the details.

A place for the community

The leading contributors appear on a new podium, with the rest of the rankings below. Equal scores share a rank. Tap a contributor to visit their profile; if you fall outside the displayed leaders, your own row stays at the bottom with your place when it is available.

Try it when the update arrives

Open Contribute → Top contributors to reach Points. It follows your app theme, adapts to larger text, and respects your phone's reduced-motion setting. Entrance animations settle instead of running continuously.

This page ranks approved contributions. Learning XP remains separate, and pending submissions do not add to the board. Scores and rankings need an available connection or previously loaded data; a failed read is labelled rather than presented as an empty score.
