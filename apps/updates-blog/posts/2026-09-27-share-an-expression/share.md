## WhatsApp / community announcement

Do you speak Kasem? You can now help in about five minutes.

Share one everyday expression — a greeting, a blessing, an idiom or a saying —
with what it means, when it is said and who you learned it from. A Kasem-speaking
reviewer checks it before anyone else sees it, and you can follow its status:
waiting for review, published, or the reviewer's reason if it needs a fix.

Approved expressions are published whole and credited to you. They are never
split into dictionary words. It is a volunteer campaign, and you can withdraw an
expression at any time.

Start here: https://indigenworld.com/contribute
