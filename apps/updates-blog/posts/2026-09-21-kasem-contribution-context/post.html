Kasem sayings carry more than a short English meaning. The contribution form now gives people separate places to record the original saying, what it means, its literal wording, when it is used, and a French meaning when one is known.

Contributors can add this detail when sending an idiom or proverb from the Indigen World app. The literal reading, usage context and French meaning are optional. Leaving a field empty is better than inventing an equivalent. The form still asks who the knowledge came from and requires permission to share it for review.

The review workflow keeps those details with the submission. Once a dictionary contribution is approved for publication, the Indigen World app, Indigen World website and Kasem Dictionary website can show the added context beside its source, pronunciation and examples. New collection records also identify which corpus area they serve, separately from the shelf where they appear.

A published entry is labelled according to the review evidence recorded for it. Approval in the current collection workflow is shown as community reviewed; it does not claim expert authentication. Older entries without a recorded authentication level remain labelled as published entries.

This change is implemented in the repository and awaits app, Functions and website releases. Existing entries do not gain missing context automatically. Specialist grammar, dialogue, pronunciation and Kawuri instruction records use their own workflows; this contribution form does not yet collect all ten corpus areas.
