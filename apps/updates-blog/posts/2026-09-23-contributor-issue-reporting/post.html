Invited contributors can report problems with translations, assignments, saving, accounts or other parts of the workspace.

Report and track a problem

Open Help & support in your assignment, choose Report an issue, select an issue type and describe the problem. The menu contains assignment guidance and Report an issue. Assignment guidance is also available in this compact menu. The report includes your assignment and current expression IDs. Bank details and unsaved answers are not automatically included. After sending, keep the reference number and use the report list shown after submission to refresh replies and status updates.

For administrators

The Contributors section has an Issues tab showing the latest 200 reports. Administrators can reply and move reports through Open, In progress and Resolved. Replies are visible to the contributor who sent the report.

Availability

Implemented locally; deployment and authenticated production verification are pending. The local preview simulates report creation only and does not contact administrators. Updates require manual refresh; email and push notifications are not included.
