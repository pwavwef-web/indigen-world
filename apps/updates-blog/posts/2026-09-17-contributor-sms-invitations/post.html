Contributor invitations can now arrive by SMS with a direct link to the contributor portal and clear sign-in instructions. This update connects the Admin contributor directory to the project's existing SMS service.

Invite from the contributor directory

In Admin, open Publishing → Contributors, choose a contributor and select Invite. Check their email and SMS phone number, add the first expression assignment, and choose Invite by SMS. Ghana numbers can be entered locally, such as 0241234567, or with their country code. Other countries require a country code.

The invitation saves the assignment, enables editing and submission, and sends an SMS containing the portal link. Contact information and registration notes remain private.

A guided first sign-in

For a newly created account, the contributor signs in using the invited email address and their phone number in international format, including the plus sign, as a temporary password. They then choose and confirm a new password before they can save or submit expressions.

People who already have an Indigen World account keep their existing password. Their SMS explains this. The first contributor activation still asks them to choose a password; already activated contributors are not reset by a resent invitation.

Clear delivery status and safe retries

The Admin panel distinguishes an SMS accepted by the provider from a failed or unconfirmed attempt. Provider acceptance does not confirm delivery to the handset. If sending fails, the account and assignment remain saved. Resend invitation retries the SMS without creating another assignment or resetting the password.

Availability

The update was deployed on 17 September 2026. Actual receipt on a contributor’s handset has not yet been verified. SMS delivery depends on the configured Arkesel sender, available SMS credit and mobile-network delivery. International delivery depends on the provider's routing. The invitation is sent as SMS; it does not use WhatsApp. Preparing or deploying this update does not automatically invite everyone in the directory.
