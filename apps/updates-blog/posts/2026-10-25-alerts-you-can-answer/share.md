Indigen World 0.1.35 for Android: your phone's notifications now look like the app's. ## Longer (Facebook / LinkedIn)

The next Indigen World app update for Android makes notifications something you can act on:

• Each alert shows who it is from and the picture it is about, with a small coloured badge for what happened, just like the notifications screen in the app.
• Reply, Like or Mark as read from the notification itself. Messages appear as a conversation you can answer without opening the app.
• Several alerts fold into one tidy group with a "Mark all as read" button.

Also included: shortcuts when you press and hold the app icon, messages you have not finished are kept as drafts, any message can be copied, deleted notifications can be brought back with Undo, and alerts can be snoozed for an hour, eight hours or until morning.
