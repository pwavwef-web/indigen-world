Contributing natural Kasem expressions needs room to think, save a draft and respond to feedback. Our latest TribeStudio work brings those steps into a dedicated workspace for invited expression contributors.

AvailabilityThis workspace is for invited contributors with assigned expressions. It is not an open sign-up programme.

Your expressions, in one place

After activating your invitation and signing in, you can open your assigned English expressions, write a natural Kasem translation and add alternative Kasem phrasings when they are useful. If you have more than one assignment, use Your assignment to switch between sets.

Search and the Not started, Drafts, Submitted and Needs revision filters help you find the next expression to work on. On a phone, the expression list and editor have separate views, with a way back to the list.

Save your work and recover unfinished writing

Drafts save automatically as you work, and the editor shows whether a save is still in progress or has failed. A saved draft is available when you sign in again. If a save fails, your text stays in the editor so you can retry.

The editor also keeps a recovery copy of unsaved text in your browser when browser storage is available. After a refresh, you can choose to restore or discard that copy. If the same expression changes on another device, the workspace asks you to reload and compare your recovery copy with the saved version.

Before closingCheck that your draft has saved. If the editor warns that browser recovery is unavailable, keep the page open until saving succeeds.

Follow a contribution through review

Submit and next sends your expression for review and opens the next expression you have not started. Submitted work has its own status, so you can distinguish work awaiting review from approved work and expressions that need attention.

When a reviewer asks for changes or rejects an expression, you can read the feedback, edit your answer and resubmit it for a new review. Pending and approved submissions stay locked. Approved expressions are published to the dictionary through the review workflow.

Separate choices about AI training

Permission to use a contribution as AI-training source data is optional and off by default. Only approved contributions with that explicit permission enter the training-source collection. Contributing an expression does not automatically train a model.

Getting started

If you have been invited, use your private activation link to set up your account. Returning contributors can sign in to the contributor workspace to continue their assignments.

Thank you to the contributors and reviewers helping build a collection of Kasem expressions grounded in how people actually speak.
