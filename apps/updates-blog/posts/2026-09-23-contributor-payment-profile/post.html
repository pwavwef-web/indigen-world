The contributor workspace now keeps bank details in a separate account settings dialog, leaving more room for translation work.

Where to find your payment profile

Open Account, then choose Payment profile. You can update your bank details, check their verification status. Close the dialog to return to your assignment.

A simpler workspace

The contributor-facing Request payment form has been removed. Payment history is also removed from the contributor profile, keeping it focused on bank details and verification.

Availability

These changes are implemented locally and await production deployment. The development preview uses fictional bank and payment data; it does not send payment actions to the service. The signed-in portal remains available to invited contributors with active accounts.

Clearer progress and a quicker way to continue

The header, assignment overview and progress panel now agree on how many expressions are submitted. Approved expressions are counted separately, so submission is not confused with approval. Continue translating takes you to work needing revision first, then other unfinished expressions.

Account details when you need them

A visible Account label opens your account menu. The payment profile shows only the last four digits of your bank account number until you choose Edit bank details. Pending, verified and rejected profiles explain the next step, with administrator feedback when available.

Return to your work

The portal remembers the last selected expression for each account and assignment on this browser when browser storage is available. Assignment switching stays disabled while edits are saving. The preview now keeps saved sample drafts when switching assignments within the same session; refreshing still resets sample data.

Review before sending

Review submission opens a summary of your translation, alternatives and permissions. Choose Back to editing to make changes, or Confirm submission to send your answer. Optional AI training remains a separate choice.

Help, saving and completion

Need help opens assignment guidance and the coordinator information supplied with your assignment. Save feedback stays visible in the editor, with a nearby retry action if saving fails. On phones, the assignment selector uses the available width. Keyboard focus is easier to see, and dialogs support keyboard navigation.

When all expressions have been submitted, the completion summary separates submitted, awaiting review, approved and returned totals. Submitted does not mean approved; check back for reviewer feedback.
