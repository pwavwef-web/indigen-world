Your words deserve a welcoming place to grow. TribeStudio’s contributor space has a new visual identity: warm colours, original illustrations, clearer tasks and more room to focus on Kasem.

Release status The redesign was deployed on September 26, 2026. The live sign-in screen has been checked; a full signed-in production check is still pending. The workspace remains for invited contributors.

A warmer welcome

The Home page opens with an illustration of an open notebook, sculptural shapes and warm clay textures. “Continue my work” takes you straight into your current assignment. Submitted, awaiting review, approved and returned work each have their own clear count.

Daily tasks, points, streaks and updates stay available. The redesign changes how the workspace looks and reads; it does not change how contributions earn approval or rewards.

More doing, less reading

Task sets have their own cards with progress, a due date when one is set, and an action to start, continue or revise. Open “Read the brief” when you need the full instructions.

Help & guide is arranged into numbered topics with a short summary. Open a topic for the details, search for an answer, or use Report a problem. Links from the editor still take you directly to the relevant guidance.

Kawuri’s page pairs a new illustration with three clear prompts for instructions, context and draft checks. Kawuri offers AI suggestions about English and context; it never writes or judges your Kasem, and it does not change your work.

Made for the screen in front of you

On a laptop, blue navigation keeps your workspace sections in view. Tablets use a compact navigation rail. Phones keep the main sections close to your thumb, with More opening the remaining sections. Forms and cards adapt to the smaller screen, and the editor keeps its focused task flow.

Gentle entrance and hover effects give the pages a little movement. They respect your device’s reduced-motion preference. The artwork is compressed and bundled with the website so it does not depend on an outside image service.

How to use it

Sign in at TribeStudio’s contributor space with your invited account. Start from Home, open Tasks to browse your assignments, or visit Help & guide for an answer. First-time sign-in instructions are available under “First time here?”. If you already have an older page open, save your draft before refreshing.

An active contributor invitation and an internet connection are required. Daily tasks depend on what the team prepares. Review, reward and payment rules continue to apply as shown in your account. The generated artwork is decorative illustration, not a photograph or cultural record.
