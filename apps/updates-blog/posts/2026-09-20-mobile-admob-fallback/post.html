Indigen World is preparing a careful advertising fallback for the mobile app: community campaigns stay first, Google can fill an otherwise empty position, and paid members continue to see no advertising at all.

AvailabilityThis work is implemented locally but is not live. AdMob review, consent settings, website verification, Play disclosures and a production app release still have to be completed.

Indigen World campaigns stay first

The app already has sponsored positions in Community, Explore and Collection for campaigns placed directly with Indigen World. Those positions, their spacing and their familiar sponsored designs remain in place. When a suitable community campaign is available, it is the advert shown.

Only when that position has no eligible Indigen World campaign may Google Mobile Ads try to fill it. The two sources never appear together in one position, and the update does not add interstitials, rewarded adverts, app-open adverts or extra interruptions.

The quiet outcome is still no advert

A Google advert is requested only after membership and the required advertising privacy choice have resolved. If consent does not allow a request, the device is offline, Google has no suitable advert or the advertising service fails, the position closes and the content remains available.

Short feeds that did not previously reach an advertising position still carry no advertising. Following, searches and sensitive account, payment and contribution screens remain outside the advertising flow.

Membership removes both sources

Indigen Plus, Patron and Creator members remain ad-free. That now means both community campaigns and the Google fallback. The app waits for a signed-in member's entitlement to resolve rather than briefly treating them as a free account, and removes loaded advertising when an ad-free membership begins or is restored.

Privacy choices are part of the implementation

The app integrates Google's consent mechanism for regions where an advertising choice is required. When Google requires a permanent privacy entry point, it appears in Settings so the choice can be reviewed later. The privacy notice also explains the device, advertising identifier, approximate-region and interaction data that Google may process for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and permitted personalisation.

What remains before release

The AdMob account and app must finish review, the account owner must approve the consent-message choices, the official Indigen World domain must publish and verify its app-ads.txt record, and the Google Play Data Safety answers must be checked against the released SDK behavior. Only then can a production build be tested and released.

Until those steps are complete and verified, this is an implementation update, not a claim that Google advertising is live.
