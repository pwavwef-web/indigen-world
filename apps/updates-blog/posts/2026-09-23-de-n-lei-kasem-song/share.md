# Sharing copy for Chinedum

## Community announcement

“De N Lei — Come Learn Kasem” is in Indigen World Music. Listen, repeat, and sing along with Kasem greetings, introductions, and polite words. Open **Collection → Music** and choose the song; its lyrics appear in the player. If a line is new to you, practise it with a Kasem speaker too.

The recording and cover are credited to Indigen World. The audio metadata says it was made with Suno; the cover is an illustration. The supplied language lines have not yet had independent review.
