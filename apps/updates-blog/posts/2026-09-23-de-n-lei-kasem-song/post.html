“De N Lei — Come Learn Kasem” is available in the Indigen World Music collection. It is a three-minute call-and-response song that invites listeners to hear a Kasem phrase, repeat it, and connect it with an English meaning. The song and its cover are credited to Indigen World.

What you can hear

The song moves through greetings, asking how someone is, introductions, polite words, and phrases for learning together. A teacher voice leads and a group repeats. The chorus returns to the same question and answer so listeners can practise them more than once.

How to use it

Open Collection → Music in the Indigen World app and choose De N Lei — Come Learn Kasem. The player shows the supplied lyrics and English lines while the recording plays. You can pause, replay, and follow along at your own pace. The cover helps identify the song in the library and player.

Who it is for

New learners can use the repeated lines as an introduction to the sounds and spelling in the supplied lyrics. Families and friends can listen together, taking turns with the teacher and group parts. The song is a practice aid; ask a Kasem speaker when you are unsure about a word, meaning, or pronunciation.

Availability and credits
