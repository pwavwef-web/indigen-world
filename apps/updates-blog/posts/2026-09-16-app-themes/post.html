Indigen World now matches its websites: the app is drawn in the same navy and blue as indigenworld.com, the Kasem Dictionary and TribeStudio. The green the app was first built in is back too, as a choice for everyone. And there are four new colour themes, from the painted walls of Tiébélé to black-and-gold kente, as a thank-you to the Patron and Studio members who carry the cost of this archive.

ShippedIn Indigen World for Android, version 0.1.22. Update from Google Play; the update may take a day or two to reach every phone. Indigen Blue and Heritage Green are free for everyone. The four supporter themes come with Indigen Patron and Indigen Creator.

What is new at a glance

  • A new look: the app now uses the navy and blue of the Indigen World websites, with a new blue app icon.
  • Heritage Green is back: the app's original green, warm paper and kente gold, free for everyone.
  • Four supporter themes: Tiébélé, Kente Gold, Harmattan Dusk and Volta Aurora, for Patron and Studio members.
  • A theme picker in Settings, with a live preview of every theme in light and dark.
  • Every theme goes everywhere: pages, lessons, Kawuri, the launch screen and dark mode.
  • Sayings in the dictionary: contributed expressions now show as one whole phrase.

A new look that matches the websites

The Indigen World websites use a deep navy with a bright action blue and a touch of cyan. The app now uses the same colours, so moving between the website, the dictionary and the app feels like one place.

Indigen Blue is the new default. Buttons you press are blue, links and selected tabs are a deeper blue, and the hero panels at the top of screens like Learn use the same navy-to-blue band the websites open with. At night, the app uses a navy ground rather than a grey one. The app icon and the launch screen are blue too.

Heritage Green, back for everyone

If you liked the app the way it was, you can have it back. Heritage Green is the palette Indigen World was first built in: warm cream paper, a deep heritage green, kente gold for the highlights, and a charcoal with a green undertone after dark. These are the original colours, with one change: the palest grey text is a shade darker, so small print is easier to read.

Heritage Green is free, and always will be.

Choosing a theme

Open your profile, go to Settings, and tap Theme. Each theme is shown as a small picture of the app in its own colours: the hero band, a card and a button. Tap a free theme and the whole app changes straight away.

At the top of the screen you can switch between Device, Light and Dark. Every preview repaints when you do, so you can see what a theme looks like at night before you choose it. The appearance and theme settings work together: pick Heritage Green and Dark, for example, and you get Heritage Green at night.

TipChoose Device and the app follows your phone: your theme in its light colours by day, and its dark colours when your phone switches to dark mode.

Your theme is kept on your phone. If you use Indigen World on more than one phone, choose it on each.

Four themes for supporters

Indigen World is kept running by the people who subscribe: they pay for the servers, the recordings and the review work behind the archive. These four themes are a small thank-you to the members who carry the most of that cost.

Tiébélé

Oxide red, charcoal and chalk: the three earths that Kassena women paint their compound walls with in Tiébélé and across the Kassena homeland. Warm chalk-white pages by day, and a deep charcoal at night.

Kente Gold

Black and gold, with a thread of kente green. In daylight the buttons are black cloth with gold lettering; at night the whole app sits on black, and the buttons turn gold.

Harmattan Dusk

Plum and amber: the savanna sky in the evening, when the harmattan dust settles. Soft rose-white pages by day, and a deep plum at night.

Volta Aurora

Violet night over the river, lit with teal. Pale lilac pages by day, and a deep violet ground at night.

Who can use them

  • Indigen Patron and Indigen Creator members (Studio members) can use all four.
  • Indigen Plus does not include them.
  • Everyone can preview them. Tap one in Settings → Theme to see it, with a link to the membership plans.

If a membership ends, the app goes back to Indigen Blue. Your choice is remembered, so your theme comes back on its own if you rejoin.

NoteA theme only changes how the app looks. Nothing you can read, hear, learn or contribute is locked behind a theme, and it never will be.

Every theme goes everywhere

A theme is not just a button colour. It changes:

  • every page, in light and dark mode;
  • the hero panels, like today's lesson on the Learn tab;
  • Kawuri, the Indigen World guide, which always has a night-time look, now in your theme's colours;
  • the launch screen you see while the app opens;
  • links and @mentions in community posts, the ring around a member's Kasem name, and progress bars on videos.

Every theme was checked for readability. In every theme, in light and in dark mode, body text, links and button labels meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio recommended for accessible reading. The app's tests check this automatically, so a later change cannot quietly make something hard to read.

Also in this update

  • Sayings in the dictionary. When someone contributes a whole expression, like a greeting or a short saying, it now shows as one phrase. Before, a comma in the middle could split it into separate words.
  • Links and mentions in community posts use your theme's accent colour.
  • The membership plans now list the app themes among what Indigen Patron and Indigen Creator include.
  • Collections: Music and Literature have their own colours on the Collections tab, so the tiles are easier to tell apart.
