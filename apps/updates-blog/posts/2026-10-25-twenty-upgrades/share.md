## Short (WhatsApp / X)

Indigen World 0.1.36 for Android brings 20 upgrades: like songs and find them in your Library, a sleep timer, replies that quote the message they answer, recent lookups in the Kasem dictionary, practice from English to Kasem with a daily reminder, and a way to undo a block. 👉 https://updates.indigenworld.com/2026/10/twenty-upgrades-from-songs-to-messages.html

## Longer (Facebook / LinkedIn)

The next Indigen World app update for Android is twenty improvements in one:

• Music: like songs and keep them in your Library, Play next and Add to queue, a sleep timer that fades out, playback speed, and readings that remember where you stopped.
• Dictionary and Learn: the words you looked up are one tap away, any word can be shared with a link anyone can open, review cards can go from English to Kasem, and a daily reminder (off until you turn it on) skips the days you have already practised.
• Messages: reply to a particular message, see when it has been read, mute a conversation, type Kasem letters, search your conversations and scroll back past the newest hundred messages.
• And more: undo a block or a mute, choose your reading size, share songs and works, and free up space taken by cached pictures.
