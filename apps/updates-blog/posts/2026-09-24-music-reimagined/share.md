## WhatsApp / community announcement

Music in Indigen World has been rebuilt.

Open Collection → Music and it now has its own navigation bar: Home, Search,
Artists and Library. The Music home takes its colours from the song that is
playing, with a band of Kassena wall-painting that moves while the music
plays.

Tap the player bar and the full player grows out of it. Swipe the cover to
change song, and tap Read along to see a song's words in large type while it
plays.

Audiobooks work the same way. Update Indigen World from Google Play. It may
take a day or two to reach your phone.
