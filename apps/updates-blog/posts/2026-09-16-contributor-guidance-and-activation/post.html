Room to say “I’m not sure”

Invited Kasem expression contributors have an update prepared for Tribe Studio: a way to flag unclear expressions and an account activation step. Production availability still needs to be confirmed.

Skip an unclear expression without guessing

Choose Skip / I’m not sure to save a flag and continue to the next new expression. Any draft stays saved, but no answer is sent for review. Use the I’m not sure filter to return later. A failed save keeps the expression open so you can retry. Expressions already awaiting review or approved remain locked.

Activate your contributor account

For newly created contributor accounts, first sign in with your invited email address and your phone number as the temporary password, including the country code. Then choose and confirm your own password to complete activation before contributing. Use your chosen password on later visits. This contributor flow does not offer Google sign-in.

Existing accounts keep their current passwords. Phone numbers are not verified by SMS. Access remains invitation-only, and the administrator assignment screen is still separate future work.
