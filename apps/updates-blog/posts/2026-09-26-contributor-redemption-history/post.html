Contributors now have a History tab in the Points section of their workspace. It brings airtime and mobile data requests together in one place, with a search box and a status filter.

Each request shows its ID, reward type, network, recipient number, points used, cedi value, date and current status. A delivery reference appears when one has been recorded. Contributors can distinguish a request waiting for review from one that has been approved, recorded as delivered, or rejected. They can use Refresh to check for an updated status.

To use it, open Points and choose History. Search by request ID or recipient, or filter to Pending, Approved, Delivered or Rejected. Airtime and data delivery still requires the team to review and fulfil requests; the History tab does not send rewards automatically. This update is implemented in the repository, with deployment and live verification pending.
