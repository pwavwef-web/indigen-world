A saved translation still needs to be submitted before the Review Desk can see it. We have made that final step easier to find in the contributor workspace.

Sharing permission is now in the open

The expression editor shows a clearly labelled Permission to submit panel. The required sharing statement is always visible, with a larger checkbox and a separate label for optional AI training.

Read the sharing statement and tick it if you agree. AI training is your choice and is not required to submit an expression.

A clear button to finish your contribution

The bold blue Submit for review button sits directly after the permissions on larger screens. On phone-sized screens it stays at the bottom while you scroll through the expression.

If a required field is missing, tapping Submit brings that field into view. The next screen lets you check your translation and permissions before you choose Confirm submission.

How to send a saved draft

  1. Open your assignment and select Drafts.
  2. Open an expression and check that its Kasem translation is complete.
  3. Read and choose the required sharing permission. Decide separately whether to allow AI training.
  4. Tap Submit for review, review your answer, then tap Confirm submission.

Your draft continues to save automatically as you edit. Submitting sends it for review; reward points depend on approval under the existing contribution rules.

Availability

This update was deployed to the TribeStudio web contributor workspace on September 26, 2026. Refresh an open workspace to load it. The mobile layout applies when you use the website on a phone; it does not change the native mobile application.
