Indigen World 0.1.37 for Android brings 20 community upgrades: labels that open a topic page, events with a date and a reminder, photo descriptions, drafts, a pinned post, muted words, and sharing a post as a picture for your status. 👉 [PUBLISHED ARTICLE URL]

## Longer (Facebook / LinkedIn)

The next Indigen World app update for Android is twenty improvements to the Community tab:

• Find things: tap a label like Questions or Events to see every post with it, jump back to the top, see where your last visit ended, and mute words you would rather not read.
• Write: describe your photos for people who cannot see them, save drafts for later, give an event its date and place, and let your phone remind you an hour before.
• Pass it on: share a post as a picture for a WhatsApp status, send it into a conversation, and ask Kawuri to explain the Kasem in it.
• People and communities: replies can be ordered, the person who asked is marked Author, pin a post to your profile, see who follows you and who you know in common, spot new posts in your communities, and search members and saved posts.

The update is built and tested; it reaches the Play Store soon. Read more: [PUBLISHED ARTICLE URL]
