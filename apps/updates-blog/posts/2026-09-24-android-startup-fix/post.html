An Android update is being prepared for testers whose Indigen World app closes while the phone's opening screen is still showing. Version 0.1.26 fixes a release startup problem that affected version 0.1.25 on the Google Play testing track.

What changed

The app's Android startup now keeps the component it needs to open its local background-work database. In 0.1.25, the release build removed part of that component, so the app could close before the Indigen World screen appeared. The fix is in the next Android app build; it does not change your account, collection, or the new Music screens.

Who this helps

This is for Android testers whose app started closing at the phone's splash screen after updating to 0.1.25. It also prevents the same startup failure on a fresh installation of the corrected version.

How to get back in

When version 0.1.26 appears on your Google Play testing track, update Indigen World there and open it as usual. You do not need to find a setting in the app to turn this fix on.

Availability: The corrected Android bundle has been built for Google Play testing. This post should be published only after the update is available on the intended testing track. Google Play may deliver an update to different phones at different times.
