Contributors can now see a Streak tab beside Tasks and Points in their portal. The three sections appear as compact status cards, showing assignment progress, available points, and the current streak.

Submitting at least one new expression on a UTC day counts toward the streak. Consecutive days increase it by one; submitting several expressions on the same day does not. A missed day resets the current streak when the contributor next submits. The Streak tab shows the current and best streak and a seven-day view. It also tells contributors whether they have completed an expression today.

This change is implemented in the repository and awaits deployment. The streak starts tracking from new submissions after deployment; earlier activity is not backfilled.
