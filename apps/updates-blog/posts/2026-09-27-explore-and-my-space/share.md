## WhatsApp / community announcement

Indigen World 0.1.28 is here.

Explore now keeps your place. Switch topics or leave for a moment, and you come
back to the reel you were watching. The feed no longer shuffles as it loads,
swiping back is instant, and you can double-tap a reel to appreciate it.

My Space is simpler: You, Membership and Settings. Your profile, everything
you have kept and everything you have given the archive are on one page.

Translate a word is now the first card on Contribute, and your saved words
follow your account to a new phone.

Update Indigen World from Google Play. It may take a day or two to reach your
phone.
