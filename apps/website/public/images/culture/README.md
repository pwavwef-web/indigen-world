# Cultural photographs in the homepage banner

Selected and verified October 12, 2026. These are documentary photographs,
not AI-generated images and not photographs of an Indigen World event.
The captions distinguish Ghanaian craft traditions from Kassena architecture
in Burkina Faso. No relationship with or endorsement by the photographers or
people depicted is implied.

All four photographs and the resized WebP derivatives are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each image has an 800px version and a 1600px version, except the kente image,
whose larger source thumbnail is 1280px. Files retain the original aspect
ratio. Changes: orientation normalised, metadata removed, resized and WebP
compressed. The webpage adds responsive cropping, a dark overlay and gentle
movement; the photographic content is not altered.

## Map

`africa-ghana.svg` contains 51 African country outlines from the public-domain
[Natural Earth 110m country dataset](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson),
projected equirectangularly and styled for Indigen World. Ghana is highlighted;
the northern Ghana marker is indicative, not a boundary of the Kasem language
or a person's location. The map's three story cards describe one pilot, not
three active country projects.
