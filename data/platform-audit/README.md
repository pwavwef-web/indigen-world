## Main findings

1. The generic Admin export path does not enforce its displayed cultural-clearance claim. It shallow-copies returned records and deletes only two field names; this is distinct from the governed corpus release export. Fix it through a trusted, per-purpose, allowlisted and audited operation, with denial tests. No private export was run to demonstrate the issue.
2. Mobile environment documentation contradicts explicit emulator opt-in in code. Flavours/registrations do not prove separate backends. Prove test startup cannot fall through to production writes and make the environment obvious.
3. Dated release receipts establish substantial deployment, but signed-in contribution/review transport, Play rollout and physical-device acceptance still need specific evidence. An AAB/GitHub release and a reachable page shell do not establish these.
4. Corpus category policies, qualified reviewer grants/quorum, source rights, consent, retention and independent speaker evaluation are operational/human gates. Third-party assistance and retrieval are not a trained Kasem model.
5. Quality reward/worker activation and production finance reconciliation remain pending in reviewed receipts. Actual settlement and economic sustainability are unmeasured here.
6. Everyday non-campaign posts intentionally publish without review. Cultural authentication labels, moderation/report/takedown operations and support staffing must reflect that separate path.
7. The candidate reader remains gated in the dated rollout record: active triggers do not mean completed backfill/readiness or enabled reading. Scaling bounds remain explicit.
8. Historical CI jobs failed before any steps because of a billing restriction. Current workflow files and local results must not become a claim of green remote CI.
9. Older publication/status/hosting/ownership notes conflict with newer receipts or code. Keep dates, source identity and the specific controlling evidence visible.
10. No verified adoption, retention, learning gains, complete rights coverage, moderation service level, settled rewards or financial sustainability dataset was queried. These are unknown, not zero or positive by implication.
