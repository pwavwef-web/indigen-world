Kasem word sheets — nouns

Batch 1 · twenty words · one word to a page · keep this page beside you while you write

How to fill in a sheet

  1. Read the English word at the top, then read the sentence under it. The sentence is there to show which meaning is being asked about — “water” the thing you drink, not “water” the garden.
  2. Write the Kasem word on the first line, large.
  3. For each form, say the whole phrase out loud first, then write down exactly what you said. Do not try to work it out as grammar.
  4. Write the whole phrase, not only the part that changed. The word that goes with a noun changes to match it, and that is most of what these lines are for — writing only the noun throws it away.
  5. Fill in Meaning 1. Use Meaning 2 only if the word really means a second, different thing.
  6. Any line you are not sure about, leave it empty. An empty line is honest. A guess becomes a dictionary entry that teaches somebody the wrong thing.
  7. Sign the bottom and write where you are from. If more than one person worked on a sheet, write both names.

If you run out of room

Write on the back of the sheet, and put the sheet code (N01, N02…) at the top of what you write there.

When the batch is done

Keep the sheets in order and send them back together. Do not throw away a sheet you could not answer — an unanswered word is something worth knowing too.

Tone marks

They sit on top of the letter before them. Only mark tone where it changes the word.

àlow
āmid
