# Original community banner artwork

Created October 13, 2026 using the built-in ImageGen tool (generation and edit).
These are AI-generated editorial illustrations of fictional people, not named
community members, photographs, or a record of an actual event. The owner chose
original artwork with the broad cut-paper approach of the Endangered Languages
Project banner. No ELP characters, artwork, video frames or media were used as
an image input or included in the website.

## Saved assets

- `community-characters.png`: initial transparent six-person master, 2048 × 768.
- `community-characters-expanded.png`: final transparent ten-person master,
  2172 × 724, used on the website.
- `paper-landscape.png`: separate opaque landscape master.
- Website assets: `apps/website/public/images/culture/original/community-characters-v2.webp`
  (229,132 bytes) and `paper-landscape.webp` (146,494 bytes). Sharp converted
  these at quality 88 and alpha quality 100; transparency is retained.

The shared character sheet is reused in every scene. CSS masks emphasize
different groups; clouds, birds, foliage and the people layer drift gently.
This is a layered illustrated banner, not a character-animated video.

## Prompt set / production brief

1. **Character generation:** Original editorial cut-paper collage of six
   fictional West African people in three seated pairs, sharing a story,
   reading a book, and writing in a notebook. Elder woman in gold with a young
   man in cream; indigo-clothed woman with headwrap and a rust-clothed child;
   sage-clothed woman and mustard-clothed man. Expressive faces, textured paper,
   hand-cut shapes, restrained detail. Wide transparent sheet with room around
   each group. No text, logos, copied characters, real-person likenesses,
   scenery or opaque background.
2. **Landscape generation:** Original wide cut-paper landscape. Cream sky
   occupies roughly the upper three quarters. Low ochre and terracotta hills
   along the bottom, sage and teal leaves at the edges, small acacia, soft
   paper grain and layered edges. Quiet open space for dark website text.
   Opaque background. No people, text, logo, birds or clouds; those are separate
   layers.
3. **Character edit:** Preserve the original six people, palette and paper
   treatment. Add a grey-haired elder man in sage, a teenage girl in rust with
   a notebook, a school-age boy in cream, and an adult woman in indigo holding
   a microphone or recording device. Arrange ten people in five close seated
   pairs sharing stories, books, notes and a recording. Recognizable faces,
   natural hands, balanced wide composition, no text or logos, no ambient glow,
   truly transparent background.
