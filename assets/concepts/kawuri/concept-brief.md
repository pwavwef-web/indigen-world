# Kawuri and Indigen World character concept brief

## Product anchor

Kawuri is the calm in-app guide for Indigen World: warm, direct, unhurried, honest about uncertainty, and respectful of community authority. The current visual mark is a gold `✣` core inside slow counter-rotating gold and terracotta rings on heritage green.

## Production goal

Explore four compact whole-body mascot directions. Each must remain readable at roughly 58 px as an avatar and later animate cleanly inside a 192 × 208 sprite cell: idle, wave, jump, success celebration, waiting, focused work, review, setback, directional movement, and a 16-direction gaze loop.

Use a polished 2D sticker / soft-vector illustration style: substantial silhouettes, rounded geometry, restrained highlights, clean edges, and no tiny decorative detail. Use the app palette: heritage green `#0B3D2E`, savannah green `#155B43`, kente gold `#D89B1D`, terracotta `#B65A3A`, plaster cream `#F7F3E8`, and ink `#1E2522`.

## Four directions

1. **Orbit Guide** — the closest evolution of the current Kawuri orb. A round heritage-green body with the gold `✣` as its face/core, terracotta-and-gold ring forms integrated into the body rather than floating, small expressive arms and grounded feet. Calm, wise, unmistakably digital, strongest continuity with the existing UI.
2. **Weaver Bird** — a compact friendly bird with deep-green plumage, warm cream face/chest, gold brow and beak, terracotta wing panels, and a subtle `✣` chest accent. Curious head tilts and wing gestures make it highly expressive. Do not imitate a real ceremonial object or specific textile pattern.
3. **Story Seed** — a rounded seed/pod character in heritage green and terracotta with one soft gold sprout, cream face, sturdy feet, and a tiny integrated `✣` mark. Represents language kept alive and knowledge growing; youthful but not childish.
4. **Ember Keeper** — a compact warm ember/lantern creature: terracotta lower body, gold flame-shaped crown integrated into the head, deep-green mantle/body accents, cream face, simple arms and feet, and a small `✣` core. Feels like patient firelight and makes celebrations visually joyful without looking frantic.

## Concept-sheet composition

Create one square 2 × 2 presentation sheet on a flat plaster-cream background. One centered full-body character in each quadrant, all at the same scale, neutral three-quarter/front pose, ample separation. No labels, words, borders, scenery, props, shadows, glows, sparkles, confetti, or detached effects. Order: Orbit Guide top-left, Weaver Bird top-right, Story Seed bottom-left, Ember Keeper bottom-right.

## Cultural and brand guardrails

Do not invent Kasem words, names, symbols, clothing, sacred motifs, or claims of cultural authenticity. Avoid generic “African” decoration, kente imitation, masks, headdresses, tribal motifs, drums, stools, gourds, architecture, readable logos, and UI screenshots. The palette and the existing abstract `✣` orbit are the only brand cues to carry forward.
