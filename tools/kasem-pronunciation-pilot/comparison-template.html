What this trial can tell us

These are unverified AI candidates. Gemini does not list Kasem in its supported languages; successful audio generation does not prove a correct pronunciation. Agreement between models is not enough to approve a word. A fluent speaker must check its meaning, tone, vowels, consonants and dialect.
